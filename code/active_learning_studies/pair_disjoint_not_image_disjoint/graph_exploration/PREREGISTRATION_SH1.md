# Pre-registration SH1 (frozen before any confirmation seed) — selection × Laplacian learner × HTR

Written 2026-10-13 after the development screens sdev1–sdev6 (seeds 8200–8219; see `PREREGISTRATION_SH0_development_plan.md`). Confirmation seeds: **confirmation 1 = 8300–8334**, **confirmation 2 = 8350–8384** (35 each, splits A and B). No confirmation cell exists at the time of this commit. Test lists are generated by `sh_tables.py confirm` from `SH1_spec_learner.json`, `SH1_spec_select.json`, `SH1_spec_select_st4.json`.

## What development showed (observations on the 20 development seeds, optimistic)
1. **Selection on top of `lap3_k10` is ≈ null** on AUC/accuracy (gain over random selection ≤ +0.006 AUC for every rule, none corrected-significant); the learner change itself is worth +0.016–0.018 AUC. The graph-aware selectors written for this study (`gdens_unc`, `gcover`, `gcover_dec`, `gvopt_node{1,3,10}`, `vopt_bridge`) were not better than `vopt_u`; their shuffled controls were equally good.
2. **HTR-directed selection moves budget, not accuracy**: `vopt_htr4` puts 41 % of the picks on HTR (random 25 %) and improves HTR log-loss by ≈ +0.015–0.025 and HTR accuracy by ≈ +0.004–0.005, while (1x1) / c(6x2) accuracy fall by up to 0.006 / 0.018 (split B).
3. **The Laplacian learner needs a longer head schedule**: training steps ×2–×4 on top of the repository's per-budget schedule improve `lap3_k10` monotonically up to ×4 (AUC +0.007…+0.010 (A), +0.004 (B); log-loss +0.03…+0.05; HTR accuracy +0.011; HTR log-loss +0.12), then plateau (×6, ×8 no better; λ = 10 or lr ×0.5 at ×4 equal). The same schedule change **does not** help `aw8_all` (AUC −0.003…−0.015, log-loss clearly worse); the best schedule found for `aw8_all` (`aw8_all_st2lr0.5`: steps ×2, lr ×0.5) is still ≈ 0.02 AUC below `lap3_k10_st4`. At ×4 the shuffled-graph and noisy-graph versions are far worse than the real graph. (Per-type Laplacian weight, HTR anchor weight ×2/×4, and lr/steps ×0.5 were not better and are dropped.)
4. The development sweeps were run on 8–12 of the 20 seeds for the last learner screen (`sdev6`, stopped early for time); the choice ×4 rests mostly on `sdev5` (20 seeds: ×2, ×3, ×4).

## Frozen candidates and tests
**Learner family** (random selection, identical label sets; candidate learner `lap3_k10_st4` = `aw8_all` + Laplacian λ = 3, k = 10, with the head's training steps × 4; `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` = best development schedule for the Laplacian-free `aw8_all`):
- Main tests: candidate vs `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` (reference 1, **primary**) and vs `lap3_k10` (reference 2, secondary family) × 2 splits × {AUC, accuracy}; Holm within each reference's family of 4.
- Control: `lap3_k10_st4_shuf` (shuffled graph, same schedule): uncorrected, AUC and accuracy in both splits.
- Success (per reference): all four differences positive, ≥ 3 with Holm p < 0.05, and better than the shuffled control (positive, uncorrected p < 0.05 for AUC or accuracy in ≥ 1 split).
- HTR family (Holm within family): `htr_acc`, `htr_ll` × 2 splits vs each reference. Reported with the largest loss of any other type's accuracy; an "HTR improvement" is claimed only if the HTR test is Holm-significant in both splits and the other types lose ≤ 0.01.
- Reference `aw8_all` with the repository schedule is also run (descriptive: total gain of the recipe).

**Selection family** (final learner `lap3_k10`, as in development; `SH1_spec_select.json`; selectors paired with random selection on the identical initial model and pool, random = mean of 3 draws): 4 candidates `vopt_u`, `gvopt_node3` (control `gvopt_node3_shuf`), `typed_decisive_bald` (control `typed_decisive_bald_shuffled`), `vopt_htr4`.
- Main family (Holm over 4 candidates × 2 splits × {AUC, accuracy} = 16): success of a candidate = all four of its differences positive, ≥ 3 Holm p < 0.05, and (graph rules only) better than its own control (uncorrected p < 0.05 in ≥ 1 split).
- HTR family (Holm within family over 4 × 2 × {htr_acc, htr_ll}); `vopt_htr4` is the HTR-directed candidate; the largest loss of the other types' accuracy is reported.
- Secondary (exploratory, not in any success rule): log-loss and the three non-HTR types' accuracy for every test above; selection under the longer schedule (`SH1_spec_select_st4.json`, same candidates, learner `lap3_k10_st4`, confirmation seeds if time allows).
Expectation stated in advance: the selection family is expected to be **not confirmed** (development gains ≈ 0, literature suggests little residual benefit of acquisition on top of a semi-supervised learner); the learner family is the one expected to pass.
