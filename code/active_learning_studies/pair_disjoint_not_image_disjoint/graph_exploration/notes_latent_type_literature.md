# Literature notes — latent-type (Dawid–Skene) model of pairwise judgments (branch claude/latent-type-1014)

Only what WebSearch result summaries stated is recorded here; no paper text was opened (everything is "(摘要)", search-result summary). arXiv/CVF are proxy-blocked.

- **Dawid & Skene (1979), EM for observer error rates** (摘要; implementations and tutorials). Latent true class per item; every annotator has a confusion matrix P(reported | true); EM alternates between the posterior of the true class per item (E) and the re-estimated confusion matrices from the soft labels (M). Assumes responses are conditionally independent given the true class. One-coin variants use a single accuracy parameter per worker. Used here: the *annotator* is the lab's judgment process; the "item" is an image pair and the latent variable is the type membership of the two images; the confusion table is shared across the four types (pooled), because ≤ 90 labelled judgments cannot support per-type tables.
- **Raykar et al. (2010), Learning From Crowds, JMLR** (摘要). Jointly learns a classifier and annotator expertise by EM, following the Dawid–Skene observation model — i.e. the classifier's posterior acts as the item prior. Analogue here: the kNN-graph label-spreading posterior is the prior over memberships, the head is trained from the posteriors (pseudo-anchors) — the classifier-in-the-loop part is only partial (the head is not used as the prior in the E-step).
- **Zhou et al. (2003/2004), Learning with Local and Global Consistency, NIPS** (摘要). Label spreading with the normalised affinity S, closed form F* ∝ (I − αS)⁻¹ Y, soft clamping of the initial labels. Used unchanged (α = 0.9) as the prior and, with soft sources, for the leave-anchors-out diagnostic. The search returned nothing on confidence-weighted soft pseudo-labels in graph SSL (not claimed).
- **Unsure / abstain options in crowdsourcing** (摘要: Machine Learning 2017 "Crowdsourcing with unsure option"; Pattern Recognition 2023 theory). Worker may skip when uncertain; benefit depends on cost trade-offs; analysed with the anonymous-worker model and majority vote. The summaries do not show how abstentions enter an aggregation likelihood; here not_apply is modelled as an ordinary outcome of the confusion table (an extra column), which is the "separate observation" option.
- **Ties in paired comparisons: Rao & Kupper (1967), Davidson (1970)** (摘要). Threshold parameters allow ties in Bradley–Terry; Davidson's model treats a win plus a loss as equal to two draws. Our head keeps the repository's |a−b| tie term; the latent-class table only models the *frequency* of ties given memberships (tie probability high when both images are of type k), not their dependence on score gaps.

**How this differs from the rejected neighbours.** (i) *Self-training on pseudo-labels* (earlier negative): pseudo-labels came from the model's own predictions/graph prior with no new evidence. Here the posteriors of judgment images additionally multiply in the likelihood of their observed outcomes under a learned confusion table; the control `prior` (no outcome likelihood, same downstream use) isolates exactly this difference. (ii) *tie / not_apply modelling in the head* (earlier negative): modelled as extra BT terms on the head; here they enter as evidence about latent memberships whose posterior is fed back through three channels. (iii) *type coupling*: the pair factor couples the outcomes of several types of the same image pair through the single latent type of each image — this is built in, not a head-side coupling.
