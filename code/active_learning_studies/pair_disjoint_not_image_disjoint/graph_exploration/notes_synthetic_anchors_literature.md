# Literature notes — simulated RHEED / synthetic anchors (synthetic-anchor study)

Search snippets only (WebSearch, standard mode, 2026-10-14); nothing below was read in full text; "(摘要)" = from the search summary. arXiv/CVF are not fetched in this environment.

- **Kinematic RHEED geometry (摘要).** Surface truncation turns reciprocal-lattice points into rods normal to the surface; diffraction occurs where the rods meet the Ewald sphere, which is nearly flat at RHEED energies; beam divergence, energy spread and finite rod width (finite domain / terrace size) broaden the intersections into streaks; single-scattering codes give positions exactly but only qualitative intensities ([rheedium tutorial](https://rheedium.readthedocs.io/en/latest/guides/ewald-ctr-tutorial.html), [kinematical streak-pattern simulation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010465511001603), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflection_high_energy_electron_diffraction)). The renderer `rheed_sim.py` is exactly this (positions from the exact scattering geometry, Gaussian rod profiles, random surrogate rod weights); numbers such as the 15 keV wavelength 0.0994 A are my own calculation, the streak-width ~ 2 pi / domain-size relation was not confirmed from a source.
- **SrTiO3(001) reconstructions (摘要).** c(6x2), c(4x2), (2x1), (2x2) and (sqrt13 x sqrt13)R33.7 are TiO2-rich double-layer reconstructions reported for annealed / MBE-grown SrTiO3(001); half-order streaks along [100] / [110] are signatures used in MBE monitoring ([APL 105, 191901](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/apl/article/105/19/191901/596650/Surface-reconstructions-in-molecular-beam-epitaxy), [c(6x2) structure](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0707.2561)). **No source defines the lab's "HTR"**, so the HTR images of the simulator are an arbitrary surrogate (1x1 rods + weak half-order rods + Kikuchi lines) and results for HTR must not be read as physical.
- **Sim-to-real generally (摘要).** Domain randomisation trains on non-realistic, widely varied simulated data so that the network keeps structure over simulation artefacts, and fine-tuning on real data on top of it helps ([Tremblay et al. 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.06516), [Understanding domain randomization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.03239), [Lil'Log overview](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2019-05-05-domain-randomization/)); practitioner sources note rendered images that look right to humans can still activate feature detectors differently than real camera images. A real-vs-synthetic discriminator is the usual adversarial diagnostic (DANN); I did not find a standard "synthetic data utility" metric. No source tested synthetic RHEED images as anchors in a preference learner, and the search found nothing RHEED-specific on sim-to-real.
- **Consequence for this study.** The study measures the gap in the feature space the learners use (nearest-neighbour distances, real-vs-synthetic classifier, synthetic-trained type classifier on real ideal images) and then tests transfer with the real held-out endpoint, as the simulator-augmented-classifier2 protocol (`simulator_augmented_classifier2_preliminary/README.md`) requires; a high synthetic-only score would not have been evidence.
