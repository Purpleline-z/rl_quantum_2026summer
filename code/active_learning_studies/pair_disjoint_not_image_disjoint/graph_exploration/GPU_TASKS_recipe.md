# GPU / lab tasks left by the recipe study (branch `claude/recipe-20261013`)

The head recipe around `lap3_k10_st4` sits on a plateau for the knobs screened here (see `RESULTS_recipe.md`); further gains are unlikely to come from more head-side knobs on the same 168 groups. What would change the picture:

1. **New, independent preference data from new growth runs (lab, highest priority).** The test judgments of every study are re-splits of the same 168 groups on 284 frames of three runs; the red-team audit showed most test images are near-duplicates of training or anchor frames. Even 30–50 new labelled groups from a *new* run/date, kept as a fixed prospective test set, would answer questions no further CPU screen can (does the recipe, or the graph term, generalise beyond these three runs; do recipe differences below ±0.003 AUC matter at all).
2. **Batched head training on a GPU for a proper random search of the recipe space (GPU, ≈ 1–2 GPU-hours).** The head is a 512→256→5 MLP trained for 400 full-batch steps; thousands of heads can be trained in parallel with `torch.func`/`vmap` (one weight tensor per configuration × seed). Use it to (a) run the 7 knobs that were *not* screened here (anchor weight 4, widths 128/512, depth 3, tie/not_apply down-weighting, AdamW betas, gradient clipping off) and (b) a random search over *joint* settings (λ, steps, lr, width, depth, anchor weight, wd) with nested seeds (search on one seed block, confirm the top 4 on a fresh one, as here). Expect it to confirm the plateau; the point is to replace "12 one-at-a-time variants" by a joint search with a proper winner's-curse protocol.
3. **End-to-end fine-tuning of the encoder with the incumbent recipe (GPU).** The head-side plateau suggests that the representation, not the head, limits the endpoint: fine-tune the SimCLR ResNet with the anchor loss + Laplacian term (graph from the frozen features or refreshed every few epochs), with cosine decay and a lower encoder learning rate (the head-side cosine decay was neutral here, the encoder may differ). Compare against the frozen-feature `lap3_k10_st4` on the same splits and report the anchor-far stratum.
4. **Anchor-strict evaluation as a standard secondary endpoint (CPU or GPU).** Report every future recipe/learner comparison on the anchor-far stratum (both test images > 0.03 from every typed anchor) *and* with near-copy anchors removed (red-team RT2); the far stratum has only ≈ 15 % of the judgments, so use at least 35 seeds × 3 label sets.
