## 2. The model and what was built
**Model.** One latent utility function f_k per reconstruction type on the frozen SimCLR features (cosine geometry of the centred, normalised features). Prior f_k ~ GP(0, a_k k~) with the semi-supervised *deformed* kernel k~(x, y) = k(x, y) − k_x^T (I + βLK)^-1 βL k_y on the cosine-kNN graph (k = 10) of the identity-safe unlabelled image nodes; on the nodes this is exactly (K^-1 + βL)^-1, the Gaussian-process version of "RKHS penalty + β f^T L f" — the Laplacian learner's smoothness penalty as a prior (unit-tested). Test images, outer-test ideal images and any non-node image enter only through the base kernel (an RBF of width c × median distance). Likelihood = the repository's loss per type (decisive → Bradley–Terry with the confidence weight; tie → smoothed |Δ|; not_apply → softplus(f); typed ideal images of the `aw8_all` anchor set as ranking constraints with the head's weights; bad images softplus(f + 1)). Inference: Laplace approximation with Newton steps in the dual (≈ 10 steps of an m × m solve, m ≈ 150–400 observed images); hyperparameters by Laplace evidence, by conditional evidence (evidence of everything minus evidence of the anchor terms alone), by grouped cross-validation, or fixed. Predictive mean and variance of f(a) − f(b) from the Laplace posterior. Code: `new_directions/nd_gpg.py` (model), `gp_run.py` (runner), `gp_tables.py` (tables and tests), `test_graph_gp.py` (5 unit tests).

### 3.7 What only a Bayesian model gives: calibration and variance (development)
**Calibration.** The GP's native probabilities are *not* calibrated: its log-loss with the mean score is about the raw log-loss of `lap3_k10_st4` and worse than the calibrated `lap3_k10_st4` (per-type constants of the calibration study), the probit variance correction makes the log-loss worse, and per-type temperatures are needed exactly as for the MLP head (§3.2). A GP fits a scale and a shrinkage with one amplitude, so evidence or cross-validation cannot decouple them; the Bayesian route to "calibration without a temperature step" fails here, and the confirmed per-type temperature calibration carries over to the GP unchanged (calibrated GP ≈ calibrated `lap3_k10_st4`, below).
**Predictive variance.** The sd of f(a) − f(b) rises with the distance to the nearest typed anchor, but it is not an error detector:

{{TABLE:variance_dev}}

(AUROC below 0.5 means the sd is *lower* for wrong predictions: the sd grows with the score margin |mean| — images far apart in feature space have both a large margin and a large variance — and the margin itself is the informative quantity, AUROC 0.83.) A variance-aware score (mean / sqrt(1 + γ var), γ ∈ 0 … 10) lowers the anchor-far log-loss by at most 0.002 (γ = 0.4) and the anchor-near log-loss by 0.005: no anchor-far-specific benefit.

## 9. Limits
- Same 168 pair groups and 284 frames of three growth runs; the confirmations re-split the same data, 35 seeds are not 35 independent data sets; the hand-set GP, the per-type constants and the amplitude prior were chosen on re-splits of the same groups. Nothing here says the GP will generalise to a new growth run beyond what the time-blocked split (whole 100-frame blocks of a run held out, 25-frame buffer) can show.
- The GP's predictive advantage comes from a local kernel (near-duplicate lookup plus typed anchors), not from the graph; the graph deformation adds ≈ 0.003–0.005 AUC at best and only helps if the graph is right (shuffled graph: catastrophic).
- Calibration is per-type scale only; no claim about calibration under shift.
- Evidence is only as good as the pseudo-likelihood of the anchor constraints; the anchors are not a proper likelihood, which probably explains why the evidence chooses β = 0.

## 10. Literature (search-result summaries only; `notes_graph_gp_literature.md`)
Sindhwani, Niyogi & Belkin (ICML 2005) construct data-dependent (deformed) kernels that warp the RKHS to the geometry of the unlabelled data and are defined for unseen points; Kapoor et al. (NIPS 2005) learn graph-based hyperparameters by evidence maximisation (EP); Borovitskiy et al. (AISTATS 2021) give Matérn graph GPs from the Laplacian's spectrum; Chu & Ghahramani (ICML 2005) give the preference GP with Laplace evidence. The result that an evidence-tuned graph GP is not better than a hand-tuned one is a statement about this data set and this pseudo-likelihood, not about those methods.
