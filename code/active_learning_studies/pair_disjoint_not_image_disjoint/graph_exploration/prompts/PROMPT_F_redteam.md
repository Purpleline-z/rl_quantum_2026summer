**Why.** The conclusions of the last two days rest on (i) a strong "unlabelled images + Laplacian regulariser + 4× training steps" learner (`lap3_k10_st4`, ≈ +0.02 AUC over the tuned graph-free learner); (ii) the same 168 labelled pair groups re-split with new seeds; (iii) a study folder literally called `pair_disjoint_not_image_disjoint`: held-out units are *pair groups*, not images, so training and test images can be near-duplicate adjacent frames of one trajectory. A Laplacian regulariser makes neighbouring images score alike and could exploit exactly that — the gain might come from near-duplicates of test images already being in training rather than from real generalisation. Also no result was ever reproduced by an independent implementation. Quantify or exclude these risks.
