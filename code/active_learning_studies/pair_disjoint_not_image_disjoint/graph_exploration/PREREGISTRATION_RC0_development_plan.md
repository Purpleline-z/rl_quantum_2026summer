# RC0 — development plan of the recipe search (written 2026-10-13 ~16:20, before any development result was read)

Seeds: development 9300–9319, confirmation 9350–9384 (`nd_core.py`: `RC_DEV_SEEDS`, `RC_CONFIRM`). Reference = the unchanged incumbent `lap3_k10_st4` (aw8_all + Laplacian λ = 3 on the k = 10 cosine-kNN graph + 4× steps); graph-free control `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` is run on the development seeds for context only. All learners are compared on identical random label sets (budgets 10/20/40/60, development: 2 draws per budget, splits A and B), endpoint = held-out decisive preference judgments (AUC, accuracy, log-loss, per-type accuracy / log-loss), plus the red-team **anchor-far** stratum (both test images > 0.03 in 1 − cos from every typed ideal-image anchor, `anc_all_min` of `rt_a1b.py`) and the anchor-near strata (reported from the stored per-judgment scores).

## Variants (≤ 12 in total; each is one switch around the incumbent, `new_directions/nd_recipe.py`)
Tier 1 (10 single-knob variants, seeds 9300–9309): `rc_fullg` (Laplacian term over all 6–7 k edges instead of 256 sampled per step), `rc_aw16` (anchor weight 16), `rc_hinge` (anchor loss = margin-1 hinge instead of logistic), `rc_w1024` (hidden width 1024), `rc_d2` (two hidden layers), `rc_do0.3` (dropout 0.3 *active during training*; the repository trains in eval mode), `rc_wd1` (AdamW weight decay 1.0, ≈ 1/3 shrinkage over 400 steps), `rc_rk` (Rao–Kupper tie likelihood with a learned margin), `rc_mix` (pair-level mixup, Beta(0.4, 0.4), soft targets, same-type decisive rows), `rc_cos` (cosine learning-rate decay).
Tier 2 (≤ 2 further variants = pairs/combinations of tier-1 survivors, 20 seeds).
**Not screened, by choice of the 12-variant budget** (listed so that the winner's-curse accounting is complete): anchor weight 4, widths 128/512, depth 3, soft-tie targets (`rc_softtie`, implemented), down-weighting tie/not_apply rows (`rc_dn0.3`, implemented), per-type learning rates (`rc_ptlr`, implemented), AdamW betas, gradient clipping off. **Not screened because inert:** confidence-weight power (≥ 95 % of judgments have weight 1.0, the rest 0.7). Earlier studies at the *default* schedule found width 64 worse, width 1024 / wd 1e-2, 1e-3 neutral, Rao–Kupper neutral-to-negative, no-tie / no-not_apply neutral-to-negative, mixture (feature-space) mixup neutral — `RESULTS`/`DEV_SCREENS.md`.

## Shortlisting rules (fixed now)
- Tier 1 (10 seeds × 2 splits): a variant is promoted to tier 2 iff at least 3 of its 4 gains over the reference (AUC and accuracy, splits A and B; mean over budgets, draws, then seeds) are positive and their mean is > +0.002. (With 10 seeds a Holm correction over 40 tests cannot reach 0.05, so tier 1 is a filter, not a test.)
- Tier 2: promoted variants are extended to seeds 9300–9319; pairs/combinations are chosen among promoted variants whose mechanisms are plausibly additive and are run on all 20 seeds. On the 20 seeds a Holm correction over all screened variants × splits × {AUC, accuracy} is computed (shortlisting aid only).
- Freeze: at most 4 candidates (single variants or combinations), ranked by the smaller of (mean of the four gains, minimum of the four gains); candidates with an anchor-far AUC gain < 0 in both splits are flagged. A pre-registration (PREREGISTRATION_RC1) is committed **before** any confirmation seed is run.
- Reported honestly: every variant that was run, with all its development numbers (generated tables), including the per-type effects.
