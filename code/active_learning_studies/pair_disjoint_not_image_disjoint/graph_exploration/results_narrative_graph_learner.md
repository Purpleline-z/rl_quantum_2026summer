# Graph-enhanced learner on top of the tuned `aw8_all` baseline

Branch `claude/graph-learner-20261012`. Every table below is generated by `gl_report.py` from the result JSONs (`new_directions/results/gl/*`); the text around them is hand-written. Same 168 labelled pair groups as all earlier work (re-split with new seeds, **not new data**), so Wilcoxon p-values are optimistic; only the pre-registered confirmation runs on previously unused seeds count as evidence.

## 1. Answer in one paragraph
A learner that adds a **manifold (Laplacian) regulariser on a cosine-kNN graph of unlabelled images** to `aw8_all` improves the held-out preference endpoint over `aw8_all` by a small but consistent margin. The rule frozen in `PREREGISTRATION_G1.md` was applied once to confirmation 1 (seeds 7100–7134) and the three plain-Laplacian candidates (λ = 1, 3, 10) pass it; they again pass on the independent replication, confirmation 2 (seeds 7200–7234), and in the cold-start confirmation (seeds 7300–7334, 10 random initial judgments). The fourth candidate (Laplacian + type-posterior ranking, `lap3_rq3_k10`) passes confirmation 1 and the cold-start confirmation but **not** confirmation 2, so it is not robustly confirmed. Effect sizes are around +0.01 to +0.02 AUC and +0.01 to +0.02 accuracy, i.e. about the size of the earlier `aw8` gain, and the replication gains are smaller than the first confirmation's. What the data do **not** show is that the benefit comes specifically from the precise kNN structure: at λ = 1 the real graph is indistinguishable from a noise-degraded graph, and the shuffled-graph control used in the success rule is a low bar (it is far worse than the baseline). Details and limits below. Encoder-style graph models (SGC, two-layer GCN) and the type-posterior prior alone gave **no** gain in development.

## 2. What was added to the protocol (pre-registered in G0/G1)
Unlabelled-image features are used to build a graph: nodes = all trajectory images + all 154 ideal images, minus the validation/test pair-group images and the outer-test ideal images of the seed (removed by SHA-256 content hash); edges = symmetric cosine kNN on mean-centred, L2-normalised frozen SimCLR features, k fixed at 10 for the confirmations (k ∈ {5, 10, 20} swept in development). Held-out images only attach to their nearest nodes and never alter node embeddings. Everything else (head, loss, per-budget learning rate/steps, all-ideal anchors with weight 8) is identical to `aw8_all`; with all graph terms switched off the code reproduces `aw8_all` (unit test). The endpoint, splits A/B, budgets 10/20/40/60 and "identical random label sets" pairing are unchanged.

What the screen showed (observations on the development seeds, selected on the same seeds, so optimistic):
- Laplacian regularisation `lap<λ>_k10` helps for λ between 1 and 30; AUC gain rises with λ, log-loss peaks near λ ≈ 1–3 and turns negative above that. k = 5/20 were not better than k = 10.
- SGC, two-layer GCN encoders and the type-posterior prior on its own (`rq`) did not improve AUC or accuracy over `aw8_all`; the GCN/SGC variants were clearly worse at k = 10.
- No-graph controls do not reproduce the gain: the shrinkage control `ridge` and dropout-consistency `cons` are neutral or harmful. The shuffled-graph versions are much worse than the baseline.
- The noisy-graph dose-response control (topology from features with added noise) is progressively worse as the graph degrades.

## 7. How to read this (author's interpretation, hedged)
- **Pre-registered, confirmed on new seeds:** the plain Laplacian learners beat `aw8_all` on AUC and accuracy in both splits (confirmation 1; replicated by the same rule in confirmation 2 with smaller effects, and in the cold-start run). That is a statement about the endpoint in this protocol, on this 168-group data, with frozen features.
- **Not robustly confirmed:** the combination with the type-posterior ranking term passed confirmation 1 and the cold start but failed confirmation 2 (0 of 4 Holm-significant), so it is not robustly confirmed. The type-posterior prior alone, SGC and GCN gave nothing in development.
- **Graph structure vs generic regularisation (a development/descriptive point, not a confirmed one):** the shuffled-graph control is much worse than `aw8_all`, so beating it says little (it forces random image pairs to share scores). The additional noisy-graph control is the more informative one: at λ = 10 the real graph is clearly better than the degraded graph, at λ = 3 it is better on most but not all rows, at λ = 1 it is statistically indistinguishable. No-graph shrinkage or dropout-consistency did not help in development. So the evidence points toward "smoothness over a sufficiently accurate image-similarity graph helps", but it does not establish that this particular kNN construction is optimal, and I did not test other similarity graphs.
- **Log-loss:** λ = 10 hurts log-loss; λ = 1–3 is neutral to slightly better (see `secondary:ll` rows; exploratory family). The candidates were frozen on AUC/accuracy.
- **HTR (the lab's priority type):** the exploratory secondary family shows HTR accuracy gains of a few points in all three confirmation runs for all four candidates. HTR accuracy is measured on a handful of held-out pairs per split, so it is coarse; this was not part of the success rule and earlier `aw8_all` work had no confirmed HTR gain. Treat it as a lead worth a prospective test, not as a result.
- **Size of the effect:** about +0.01 to +0.02 AUC. It is comparable to what the anchor-weight change gave and does not change the qualitative picture that the learner side matters more than selection rules. Confirmation-1 gains are larger than confirmation-2 gains; the true effect is probably smaller than the confirmation-1 point estimates.

## 8. Limits
- Same 168 pair groups in every seed (re-splits), so seed-level independence is only approximate and p-values are optimistic.
- Hyper-parameters (λ, k = 10, candidate set) were chosen on development seeds; controls and the success rule were written before the confirmation seeds were run.
- Only one graph family (cosine kNN on frozen SimCLR features) was tried; no test of other encoders, other similarity measures, or end-to-end fine-tuning (see `GPU_TASKS_graph_learner.md`).
- All negative results (SGC, GCN, `rq`, `cons`, `ridge`) mean "this implementation, this schedule, no gain", not "impossible".
