# Literature notes — calibration of a Bradley-Terry preference head (branch claude/calibration-20261013)

All statements are what the WebSearch result summaries said ("(摘要)" = search-result summary only; no paper was opened; arXiv full texts are blocked by the proxy). Searched 2026-10-13.

1. **Temperature scaling with few calibration labels** (摘要). Several papers/tutorials report that plain temperature scaling is fragile when the calibration set is small or noisy (Attended Temperature Scaling, 2019 preprint; a 2026 preprint on small validation budgets which proposes a low-capacity calibrator on *cross-fitted out-of-fold* risk scores; a Eusipco 2022 paper noting that low-confidence ECE bins are estimated from few samples). A single scalar temperature is the most stable choice but cannot act differently across classes (Dirichlet calibration paper) or inputs. A tutorial notes that fitting T on the training set under-estimates the needed temperature. -> Design consequence used here: fit T on **out-of-fold** logits of the labelled judgments (K-fold by pair group), shrink per-type temperatures to the global one, report per-seed pooled ECE (binned ECE of one 60-judgment test set is too noisy).
2. **Reward-model calibration** (摘要). Bradley-Terry reward models are described as over-confident on inconsistently labelled or out-of-distribution data; the standard post-hoc fix is dividing the score gap by a temperature fitted on held-out preference pairs (NLL or ECE objective); five-member BT ensembles (mean/SD of rewards) are the usual uncertainty baseline; a process-reward-model study finds that uniform rescaling only works when all instances are consistently over/under-estimated and fails to calibrate well out of distribution. -> The anchor-far stratum below is exactly an "out-of-distribution" slice; a single T is expected to help the anchor-near majority and not the far slice.
3. **Label smoothing and ensembles** (摘要). Müller, Kornblith, Hinton (2019, "When does label smoothing help?"): smoothing alpha = 0.1 lowers ECE to about the level of temperature scaling on ImageNet (0.071 -> 0.035 vs 0.022 with T = 1.4) and weakens distillation. Roschewitz et al. (TMLR 2025) on calibration under dataset shift: entropy regularisation + label smoothing most effective for raw probabilities; calibrating ensemble members before averaging beats calibrating the averaged output under shift; improving calibration under shift can worsen in-distribution calibration.
4. **Ties in paired comparisons** (摘要). Rao & Kupper (JASA 1967) add a threshold parameter (reduces to Bradley-Terry at the neutral setting); Davidson (JASA 1970) adds a draw-propensity parameter (zero recovers Bradley-Terry); the two are different models. -> The tie / not_apply modelling variant here is Davidson's, fitted post hoc on OOF logits; it is scored on decisive judgments only (the endpoint), so ties can only matter through the shared logit scale.
