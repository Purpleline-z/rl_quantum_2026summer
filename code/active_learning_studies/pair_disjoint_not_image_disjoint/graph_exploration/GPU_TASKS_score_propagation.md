# GPU / lab tasks left by the score-propagation study

1. **New growth run, labelled prospectively (lab; highest priority).** The HTR route and every interpolation-type result here rely on neighbouring labelled / anchored frames; on the time-blocked split (no labelled frame within 25 frames of the test block) the route gives nothing. Only a new run, with a pre-registered test set of HTR-containing pair groups (even 30–50 groups), tells whether free-score propagation generalises beyond the three recorded runs.
2. **More HTR judgments and HTR anchors (lab).** The head is weak on HTR because HTR has the fewest anchors (26 of 144) and judgments; the free scores beat it there. Adding HTR ideal images or HTR pair judgments is the direct lever and would show whether the route stays useful once the head is stronger on HTR.
3. **End-to-end head with a score-propagation module (GPU).** Train the encoder + head with the free-score model as an auxiliary teacher on HTR only (distillation of HTR pair differences), refreshing the graph from the encoder every few epochs; compare against the frozen-feature HTR route.
4. **Batched exact solves (GPU, ≈ 1 GPU-hour).** The free-score Newton solve is a 1150-dimensional dense system per type column; thousands of (λ, ρ, kernel) settings × seeds can be solved in parallel with batched Cholesky, enabling a proper nested-seed search of the routing scale and the per-type choice (here chosen on 20 development seeds).
