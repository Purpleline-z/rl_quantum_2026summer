# Pre-registration SH0 (development plan) — selection × Laplacian learner × HTR

Branch `claude/selection-htr-20261013`, written 2026-10-13 before any development cell. Seeds: development 8200–8219; confirmation 1 = 8300–8334; confirmation 2 = 8350–8384 (blocks added to `nd_core.py`, asserted unused). Common rules: `prompts/COMMON_RULES.md`.

## Question
(1) With the best learner so far (`lap3_k10` = `aw8_all` + Laplacian regulariser on the unlabelled-image kNN graph; 3 pre-registered confirmations, +0.01–0.02 AUC over `aw8_all`), does choosing *which* judgments to label still beat random selection? Earlier result: under `aw8_all` the `vopt_u` advantage was not confirmed (borderline in split A), and none of 30 repository selectors was distinguishable from random on development seeds. (2) Can the lab's priority type HTR be improved by a selector or by a learner change, without costing other types?

## Harness (unchanged protocol, `new_directions/nd_select.py`)
Single-shot selection: one batch of b judgments (b = 10/20/40/60) is chosen from the model trained on the initial groups; the final learner is trained on initial + chosen judgments; selectors are paired with random selection on the identical initial model and candidate pool; random = mean of 3 draws. Endpoint: held-out pair-group AUC, accuracy, log-loss (splits A and B), per-type accuracy/log-loss (HTR from ~10 held-out decisive judgments per split — coarse; pooled over seeds only). Gains are per-seed means over budgets/draws; Wilcoxon over seeds; Holm within pre-registered families; paired bootstrap CIs.

## Protocol extension (stated explicitly)
Graph-aware selectors may use the *unlabelled* image nodes of the Laplacian learner's graph (all trajectory images + all ideal images, minus validation/test pair-group images and outer-test ideal images by SHA-256 content hash) and the typed reference images (already training anchors). They never use the outcome of any candidate or test image. A candidate image that is not a node (same content as a held-out image) is represented by its k nearest nodes. Every graph rule has a shuffled-graph control (node identities of the adjacency permuted).

## Development screens (≤ 8 selector variants + ≤ 4 learner changes are carried to the freeze)
- sdev1 (running): earlier selectors (`vopt_u`, type-aware coverage ± `typeonly`/`shuffled` controls, `typed_decisive_bald`, `gvopt_lap*`) × learners {`aw8_all`, `lap3_k10`}.
- sdev2: new graph-aware selectors under `lap3_k10` (`gdens_unc`, `gcover`, `gcover_dec`, `gvopt_node{1,3,10}`, with `_shuf` controls) and HTR-directed selectors (`vopt_htr{2,4}`: HTR head gain × w; `vopt_bridge{1,3}`: HTR/RT13 judgments weighted by label-spreading ambiguity between the two types, with `_shuf`).
- sdev3: learner changes under random and `vopt_u` selection: per-type Laplacian weight (`lap3h10_k10`: HTR weight ×3.33), steps ×0.5/×2, learning rate ×0.5/×2.
- sdev_cold: leading rules from a cold start (10 random initial judgments, `ND_INITIAL=random`).
Per-type metrics and the type composition of the chosen sets (budget spent per type) are recorded for every cell; mechanism analysis is generated by `sh_tables.py comp`.

## Freezing and success rules (to be written in PREREGISTRATION_SH1.md before any confirmation seed)
≤ 4 candidates (selector × learner), each with its shuffled-graph control (or plain `vopt_u` for non-graph rules), and the test family: candidates × 2 splits × {AUC, accuracy} vs random selection under the same learner (Holm); success = all four differences positive, ≥ 3 with Holm p < 0.05, and better than the control (uncorrected p < 0.05 in ≥ 1 split). HTR is a separate family (htr_acc, htr_ll × 2 splits × candidates; Holm within the family), reported side by side with the main family, together with the largest loss in the other three types' accuracy (non-HTR loss ≤ 0.01 required for an "HTR improvement" claim). Confirmation 2 replicates with the same rule; no re-tuning after seeing confirmation results. If nothing passes, the result is "not confirmed" plus the mechanism analysis (where the budget went).

## Expectation (stated in advance)
Literature and the earlier selection results suggest small or null average gains of selection on top of a stronger learner (see `notes_selection_htr_literature.md`); the HTR question is the more promising one but HTR test pairs are few, so any HTR claim is expected to need pooling across seeds and to be reported with wide intervals.
