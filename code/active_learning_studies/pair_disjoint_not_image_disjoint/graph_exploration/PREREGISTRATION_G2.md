# Pre-registration G2 (frozen before any confirmation seed) — regularisers on unlabelled images, graph or not

Written 2026-10-13 after the development screens (seeds 9000–9011; round 1 and round 2 below). **Confirmation seeds: 9050–9084** (35 seeds, splits A and B, each cell run once). Nothing from 9050–9084 exists at the time of this commit; the seeds 9012–9019 (the rest of the development block) were not used. Tests are generated by `rg_tables.py confirm G2_spec.json DIR [DIR ...]` from `G2_spec.json` (committed with this file).

## Question
At the long head schedule, is the Laplacian regulariser on the kNN graph of unlabelled images (`lap3_k10_st4`) needed, or does a graph-free regulariser on the same unlabelled images do as well? Motivation: the selection-htr study found, on its development seeds only, that dropout-consistency `cons0.3_st4` reached most of the gain of `lap3_k10_st4`; "the graph is needed" had only been supported by shuffled/noisy-graph controls, not by a search over graph-free regularisers.

## What the development screens did (all variants are listed; 12 seeds, one random label draw per budget 10/20/40/60, identical label sets across learners)
Interpretation of "<= 10 variants": **at most 10 variants per development round, two rounds (20 variants in total, plus controls and references)**; I did not run a single round of 10 because the requested mechanism list is longer than 10.
- Round 1 (≤ 10 variants, all at the 4× schedule): dropout-consistency (`rgcons0.3_st4`), Gaussian-noise consistency (`rgnoise1_st4`), mixup consistency (`rgmix0.3_st4`), R-Drop (`rgrdrop1_st4`, hidden dropout 0.2), training with hidden dropout 0.5 (`rghdrop0.5_st4`), weight decay 1.0 (`rgwd1_st4`), EMA of the head weights (`rgema0.98_st4`), early stopping on an inner 25 % split of the labelled judgments with retraining for the best step count (`rginner_st4`), Laplacian on the hidden layer (`rglaph1_k10_st4` + shuffled control), hard-neighbour Laplacian on the 1-NN graph (`rglap1_k1_st4` + shuffled control). References: `aw8_all`, `aw8_all_st2lr0.5`, `lap3_k10_st4`, `lap3_k10_st4_shuf`.
- Round 2 (10 variants + 1 control): refinement of the round-1 winners: `rgcons1_st4`, `rgcons3_st4`, `rgcons0.3_st6`, `rgnoise3_st4`, `rgnoise1_st6`, `rgrdrop3_st4`, `rgmirror1_st4` (mirror consistency), `rgcn_c0.3_n1_st4` (dropout + noise consistency), `rgcn_c0.3_n1_rd1_st4` (+ R-Drop), `rglap3_cons0.3_k10_st4` (Laplacian + dropout consistency) with control `rglap3_cons0.3_k10_shuf_st4`.
- Not run: manifold mixup in the hidden layer (identical to input mixup before the ReLU and an identity after it, for the repository's one-hidden-layer head with a linear output; checked in the unit tests), SWA (EMA was run instead), sweeps at 1× and 2× steps (the 4× and 6× schedules were the ones that mattered for the reference; the 1× schedule was shown not to help graph-free regularisers in the graph-mechanism study).
- Development observations (12 seeds, optimistic, not evidence): (1) the graph-free consistency-type regularisers (dropout / noise consistency, alone or combined) match `lap3_k10_st4` on AUC (differences −0.004…+0.004) but are 0.005–0.02 lower in accuracy; (2) EMA, weight decay, inner-split early stopping, hidden-layer Laplacian, hidden dropout, mirror consistency, mixup consistency (explodes; log-loss far worse), and the 1-NN "hard neighbour" Laplacian were clearly worse (−0.01…−0.09 AUC); R-Drop at weight 1 was close in AUC but worse in accuracy and log-loss, and worse at weight 3; (3) all shuffled-graph controls are far worse than the real graph; (4) `lap3 + cons0.3` (graph + dropout consistency) is +0.006 AUC over `lap3_k10_st4` in both splits and ≈ +0.004 / −0.007 accuracy (A / B); (5) **anchor-far endpoint (both test images > 0.03 from every typed ideal-image anchor; about 22 % of the test judgments on average): the AUC advantage of `lap3_k10_st4` over `aw8_all` / `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` is only +0.001…+0.004 in this stratum (accuracy advantage +0.013…+0.029, noisy: ≈ 5–20 judgments per seed), while the AUC advantage is +0.012…+0.027 in the anchor-near stratum** (the stratum split follows the red-team audit); the graph-free consistency learners F1/F2 are +0.008…+0.016 AUC *above* the reference in the anchor-far stratum in both splits (development; ≈ 5–20 judgments per seed, noisy).

## Frozen candidates, controls and tests
**Learners (random selection, identical label sets, 4 budgets × 2 draws per (seed, split) for the main learners; the two shuffled-graph controls on draw 0 only):**
- Reference graph learner: `lap3_k10_st4` (= `aw8_all` + Laplacian λ = 3, k = 10, 4× steps).
- Graph-free candidates (3): **F1** `rgcons0.3_st4` (dropout-consistency of unlabelled nodes' scores, weight 0.3, input-dropout 0.3, 4× steps); **F2** `rgcn_c0.3_n1_st4` (dropout-consistency 0.3 + Gaussian-noise consistency 1.0, 4× steps); **F3** `rgcons0.3_st6` (as F1 with 6× steps).
- Graph candidate (1): **G1** `rglap3_cons0.3_k10_st4` (Laplacian λ = 3 + dropout consistency 0.3, 4× steps).
- Controls: `lap3_k10_st4_shuf` (shuffled graph for the reference), `rglap3_cons0.3_k10_shuf_st4` (shuffled graph for G1), `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` (tuned graph-free baseline without any unlabelled-image term).
- Cost note: the 8 learners × 35 seeds × 2 splits × 4 budgets × 2 draws take ~2 h on 4 cores, which is why the controls run on one draw.

**Endpoints.** Standard held-out endpoint: AUC and accuracy of the decisive held-out judgments (weighted as in the repository), per seed averaged over budgets and draws. **Anchor-far secondary endpoint**: the same metrics restricted to test judgments whose two images are both more than 0.03 (1 − cosine of the mean-centred SimCLR features) from every typed ideal-image anchor of the seed's split (`rg_run.anchor_min_distance`, same definition as `rt_a1b.py`). The anchor-near complement is also stored. If the far stratum of a seed has only one class the seed is dropped from that test. The anchor-far tests are secondary and enter no success rule; any gain that exists only in the anchor-near stratum is reported as such.

**Tests (two splits × {AUC, accuracy}; per-seed paired differences; Wilcoxon signed-rank over seeds):**
- NI (success A): for each graph-free candidate c, one-sided non-inferiority of c against `lap3_k10_st4`: H0 median(c − ref) ≤ −margin, margin = 0.003 AUC and 0.01 accuracy; 3 candidates × 4 = 12 tests, Holm over the 12.
- SUP (success B): for each graph learner G ∈ {`lap3_k10_st4`, G1} and each graph-free candidate c, two-sided test of G − c; Holm within each graph learner's 12 tests.
- CTRL: each graph learner vs its shuffled-graph control, two-sided, uncorrected. TUNED: `lap3_k10_st4` vs `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` (re-check of the earlier confirmation), uncorrected.
- Secondary (anchor-far): the NI tests (FAR_NI, Holm over 12) and SUP tests of the reference (FAR_SUP, Holm over 12) on `auc_far` / `acc_far`.

**Success rules (decided in advance).**
- **A — the graph is not needed:** at least one graph-free candidate has ≥ 3 of its 4 NI tests with Holm p < 0.05.
- **B — the graph adds:** at least one graph learner G (the reference or G1) beats **every** graph-free candidate: for each candidate all four differences G − c are positive and ≥ 3 of 4 have Holm p < 0.05.
- If both hold: a real graph gain that is smaller than the margin (report both). If neither: **inconclusive**. A graph claim additionally requires G to beat its shuffled-graph control (positive and p < 0.05 for AUC or accuracy in ≥ 1 split); if it does not, B is withdrawn.
- Honest expectation, stated in advance: with 35 seeds the per-seed standard deviation of the paired AUC gain (≈ 0.009–0.012 in development) gives a standard error of ≈ 0.0015–0.002, which is of the order of the non-inferiority margin 0.003; I therefore expect A to be hard to establish even if the true difference is zero, and expect accuracy (gap ≈ 0.005–0.02 in development) to decide B. "Inconclusive" is a likely outcome and will be reported as such.

## Runs
```
bash scripts_rg/run_rg.sh conf lap3_k10_st4,aw8_all_st2lr0.5,rgcons0.3_st4,rgcn_c0.3_n1_st4,rgcons0.3_st6,rglap3_cons0.3_k10_st4 9050-9084 A,B 2 4
bash scripts_rg/run_rg.sh conf_ctl lap3_k10_st4_shuf,rglap3_cons0.3_k10_shuf_st4 9050-9084 A,B 1 4
python3 rg_tables.py confirm G2_spec.json ../../new_directions/results/rg/conf ../../new_directions/results/rg/conf_ctl
```
Each cell is run once; a crashed shard is restarted unchanged (idempotent); nothing is re-tuned. Limits: same 168 pair groups re-split (p-values optimistic: test judgments recur across seeds); test images are near-duplicates of training frames (red-team audit), hence the anchor-far endpoint; the development choices (weights, 4×/6× steps, which mechanisms) were made on the 12 development seeds; the Holm families are small and correlated; the learners share the same kNN graph code and the same feature extractor; torch 2.14 (CPU numerics reproduce the committed `lap3_k10_st4` / `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` cells of the selection-htr study to four decimals, checked on seed 8300 split A).
