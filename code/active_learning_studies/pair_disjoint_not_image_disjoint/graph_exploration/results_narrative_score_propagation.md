# Non-parametric score propagation vs the Laplacian head (branch `claude/score-propagation-20261014`)

Every table is generated by `sp_report.py` / `sp_confirm.py` / `rc_tables.py` from the result JSONs (`new_directions/results/sp/*`); the text around them is hand-written. Same 168 labelled pair groups as all earlier work (re-split with new seeds, **not new data**): p-values are optimistic; only the pre-registered confirmation (seeds 11230–11264, frozen in `PREREGISTRATION_SP1.md` before any of those cells existed) counts as evidence; development seeds are 11200–11219 (selected on the same seeds, optimistic). Reference = `lap3_k10_st4` (unchanged); tuned graph-free control = `aw8_all_st2lr0.5`.

## 1. Answer
**Question: how much of `lap3_k10_st4`'s gain is interpolation that a non-parametric method already provides, and does a non-parametric / hybrid model beat it?**

**Pre-registered confirmation 1 (seeds 11230–11264, 35 seeds, splits A and B, run once; section 4):**
- **A pure non-parametric model is clearly worse than the head.** Free scores (Bradley–Terry + anchors + Laplacian over all nodes, no head; `fs_lam10`) lose −0.025 / −0.026 AUC and −0.043 / −0.049 accuracy (A / B; WORSE, 4 of 4 Holm-significant), and the loss is concentrated on judgments far from every typed anchor (about −0.10 to −0.13 AUC there); on anchor-near judgments they are only −0.019 / −0.014 AUC behind. The same free scores are *better* than the tuned graph-free head in the near stratum: they recover **about half of the head's near-anchor AUC gain over the graph-free head** (share 0.48 [0.20, 0.69] in split A, 0.55 [0.08, 0.94] in split B) and nothing away from anchors. Label spreading of the anchors alone (`ls`, −0.065 / −0.063 AUC) and the nearest-scored-node baseline (`kn`, −0.052 / −0.060) are far worse; the shuffled graph (−0.39 / −0.37) and the no-penalty memorisation control (−0.23 / −0.24) collapse, so the interpolation really is carried by the graph.
- **Hybrids (a) head + free residual and (b) distillation do not help.** Residual hybrid `hy_lamr10_rhor0.1`: −0.003 / −0.003 AUC, −0.009 / −0.009 accuracy (WORSE); distilled head `ds_mu0.03_lam10`: +0.001 / +0.001 AUC, −0.002 / +0.001 accuracy (NON-INFERIOR within 0.003 AUC / 0.01 accuracy, no gain). Cross-validated all-type stacking (development only) chose noisy weights and was worse.
- **Headline: routing only the HTR judgments to the free scores is BETTER than `lap3_k10_st4`** (`rt_c2_lam10_rho0.001`: head for 1x1 / c(6x2) / RT13, 2 × free score for HTR; 3 of 4 main tests Holm-significant, all four gains positive): AUC **+0.0079 / +0.0049**, accuracy **+0.0048 / +0.0034** (A / B; accuracy in split B is significant uncorrected, p = 0.017, but not after Holm over 16 tests, 0.10); HTR accuracy +0.032 / +0.026, HTR log-loss +0.145 / +0.112. The gain is graph-specific (shuffled-graph route −0.041 / −0.044 AUC, no-penalty route −0.021 / −0.022) and exists **also on anchor-far judgments** (far AUC +0.018 / +0.004, far accuracy +0.011 / +0.009; 2 of 4 far tests Holm-significant, all four positive) — the first confirmed gain in that stratum in this project — and in the near stratum (+0.007 / +0.005 AUC). It is **independently replicated in confirmation 2** (seeds 11265–11299, headline only, SP1b): AUC +0.0088 / +0.0057, accuracy +0.0056 / +0.0034, 4 of 4 Holm-significant (Holm over 4); far AUC +0.0074 / +0.0035. Secondary evaluations on confirmation-1 seeds: cold start AUC +0.0082 / +0.0057 (accuracy +0.0053 / +0.0024), time-blocked split AUC +0.0055, accuracy +0.0043 (both Holm-significant; development had shown ≈ 0 on the blocked split, +0.0004 / −0.0006, so the blocked-split evidence is mixed across seed sets).

**What this means and does not mean.** The gain is small (+0.005–0.009 AUC, +0.003–0.006 accuracy), it comes entirely from HTR judgments (≈ 15 % of the test judgments; head HTR accuracy is already 0.93–0.98), the other three types are untouched by construction, and the routed type and its scale (c = 2) were chosen on the development seeds after looking at per-type tables (post-hoc; the confirmation seeds are fresh). The pooled-AUC gain depends partly on the scale c (c = 1 gave about 80 % of the c = 2 development AUC gain), i.e. partly on cross-type calibration; the within-type HTR accuracy gain (+0.03) does not. The head is weak on HTR because HTR has the fewest typed anchors (26 of 144) and judgments; a non-parametric model that propagates the labelled HTR judgments and anchors over the graph fills that gap, and the HTR gain is largest for HTR test images far from every labelled frame (+0.058 accuracy in the farthest tercile, +0.007 in the nearest) — so it is not memorisation of near-duplicates. Everything is on the same 168 labelled groups and three growth runs.

## 2. What was built
- **Free scores** (`new_directions/nd_propagate.py`): one free score per type for every graph node (the 1120 kNN-graph nodes plus extra nodes for typed anchors that are not graph nodes — the 28 outer-test ideal images — and for labelled images that are not nodes; held-out pair-group images are never nodes). Objective per type column (convex): Bradley–Terry likelihood of the labelled judgments (the repository's winner / tie / not_apply terms, hinges smoothed), the anchor ranking terms (weight 8, all ideal images), the bad-image term, λ × Laplacian penalty over all nodes, ρ × ridge. Solved exactly by damped Newton with the dense Hessian (matches 3000-iteration L-BFGS to 7 digits, ≈ 1.5 s per fit). **No parametric head.** Test images (never nodes) get the softmax(sim/0.05)-weighted mean of the scores of their 5 nearest nodes.
- **Baselines / controls:** `ls` label spreading of the anchors' class one-hots (no judgment used); `kn` free scores without Laplacian read out at the nearest *scored* nodes (nearest neighbour in score space); `fs_lam0` free scores without penalty (memorisation); shuffled-graph versions `*_sh1` (adjacency node identities permuted, extras attached to random nodes); `aw8_all_st2lr0.5`.
- **Hybrids:** (a) `hy_*` head `lap3_k10_st4` + free residual (penalties on the residual); (b) `ds_*` the Laplacian head with pair-difference distillation of a free-score teacher on unlabelled nodes/edges; (c) `st_*` in-budget cross-validated stacking weight (all types), and `rt_*` / `bl_*` per-type fusion (HTR judgments use c × d_free / d_head + w d_free, other types keep the head).
- **Evaluation:** standard endpoint (splits A/B, budgets 10/20/40/60, identical random label sets, held-out decisive preference judgments), anchor-far stratum (both test images > 0.03 from every typed anchor, `rt_a1b.py` definition), time-blocked contexts (`rt_blocked.py`) and cold start (`ND_INITIAL=random`).
- Development plan `PREREGISTRATION_SP0_development_plan.md` (+ amendment SP0b) was written before any development result was read; amendment SP0b after the screens and the HTR-route observation, before any confirmation seed.

### 3.10 Development observations
- **Pure free scores:** best `fs_lam10` (λ = 10, ρ = 0.01); λ ∈ {1…100}, ρ ∈ {0.0001…0.1}, anchor weight 2–32, kernel (1–10 neighbours, τ 0.02–0.2) and extra-anchor nodes change AUC by ≤ 0.01 and never close the gap; smaller ρ helps anchor-far AUC and HTR accuracy (the reason the HTR route uses ρ = 0.001); kernel settings are irrelevant. Free scores equal the graph-free head in the near stratum and fall apart far from anchors.
- **Where the free scores beat the head:** HTR only (HTR accuracy +0.01 … +0.04 for every ρ ≤ 0.01 variant); worse on 1x1, c(6x2), RT13 (accuracy −0.04 … −0.09). Routing any other type was negative (offline scan, 20 seeds).
- **Hybrids (a), (b), (c):** residual strength grid (λ_r 3–30, ρ_r 0.001–0.1, penalty on residual or on total): best = strongest ridge, never better than the head; distillation μ ∈ {0.03, 0.1, 0.3}: best μ = 0.03 ≈ head; shuffled-graph teacher worse; stacking by in-budget CV: weights drawn from the whole grid, −0.009 / −0.011 AUC (8 seeds; stopped early, ≈ 30 s per cell).
- **Time-blocked split (development, 20 seeds):** HTR route +0.0004 AUC / −0.0006 accuracy (intervals include 0); pure free scores −0.029 AUC; the graph-free head −0.021 AUC.
- **Not run in development:** cold start (confirmation only); graph variants other than k = 10 (earlier studies found none better); joint tuning of c and the free-score parameters.
- **Process notes.** The HTR route was found after the free-score / hybrid screens, from the per-type tables of the first 10 development seeds (amendment SP0b states this before any confirmation seed); the three other candidates are the best of their kind by the ranking score and were expected to be null; the distillation and stacking runs were stopped after 8 of 10 seeds because the conclusion was clear. A container restart interrupted confirmation 1 once; the runner is idempotent and finished the remaining cells with the same code. Confirmation 2 was reduced to the headline candidate by amendment SP1b, written after confirmation 1's standard endpoint was analysed and before any confirmation-2 seed.

## 5. Limits
- Same 168 labelled groups and 284 frames of three growth runs; seeds only re-split them, so seed-level p-values are optimistic and nothing here speaks to new runs. Test sets are ≈ 56–70 decisive judgments per (seed, split), of which ≈ 10 are HTR; the anchor-far stratum keeps ≈ 15 % of the judgments (4–15 per cell).
- The headline gain is small and confined to HTR judgments; it is a per-type fusion chosen post hoc on development seeds (type and scale c = 2), with a winner's-curse design (4 candidates out of ≈ 45 learners tried) mitigated by two fresh seed sets that agree.
- Free scores rely on neighbouring labelled / anchored frames (test images are near-duplicates of training frames); the time-blocked evidence is mixed (development ≈ 0, confirmation +0.0055 AUC), and no new growth run exists to test generalisation.
- Non-parametric solves are exact for the stated objective (hinges smoothed); no learned kernel, no other graph than the k = 10 cosine kNN graph, one operating point of λ, ρ, aw.
- Stacking was only screened (8 development seeds); distillation and the residual hybrid were each confirmed at one setting.
