# Pre-registration HI1 — honest (group-level) inference for the confirmed learner ladder

Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE any confirmation cell (seeds 11330–11399) exists and BEFORE any group-level interval, p-value, variance component or power number was computed for any real data. What was looked at before this freeze: run times, the cluster structure of the 168 groups (unit counts, `hi_clusters.py`), and the equivalence of the re-run predictions with the stored red-team predictions (identical to the last digit for `aw8_all` and `lap3_k10_st4`, seed 8800 split A). The estimator code was developed and validated on synthetic data only (`code_behavior_tests/test_honest_inference.py`: estimator = standard endpoint, exact additive decomposition, bootstrap coverage under group heterogeneity, exact randomisation test).

## Why
Every earlier confirmation re-splits the SAME 168 labelled pair groups (284 frames of three growth runs); the seed-level Wilcoxon p-values measure training / label-draw variability, not the uncertainty of the conclusion about new groups. HI1 replaces the seed by the pair group (and by clusters of near-duplicate groups) as the unit of inference.

## Runs (each once; `hi_run.py`, `scripts_hi/run_hi.sh`)
- Cells: (seed, split A/B, budget 10/20/40/60, label draw), identical random labelled sets for every learner (`nd_core.random_labelled`), per-judgment held-out logits `d` stored with the pair-group id, type, sign, confidence weight and the distance of the judgment's closer image to the nearest typed anchor (`anc_all_min`, as `rt_a1b.py`).
- Development: seeds 11300–11319, 3 draws per budget, learners `baseline, aw8, aw8_all, aw8_all_st2lr0.5, lap3_k10, lap3_k10_st4, lap3_k10_st4_shuf, lap3_k10_st4_rt13off, gmc_t_no_rt13` (already launched). Development data are used for code checks and are analysed afterwards as a third, secondary replicate; they do not enter the pooled estimate.
- Confirmation 1 = 11330–11364, confirmation 2 = 11365–11399: 2 draws per budget, learners `baseline, aw8, aw8_all, aw8_all_st2lr0.5, lap3_k10, lap3_k10_st4, lap3_k10_st4_shuf, gmc_t_no_rt13` (`lap3_k10_st4_rt13off` is dropped for compute; development only). `lap3_k10_st4_cal` = `lap3_k10_st4` with each judgment's logit divided by the per-type temperature of the calibration branch (types 0 / 2 / 3 / 4: 1.352 / 0.682 / 0.293 / 0.630, constants of `CAL1_spec.json`, fitted on development seeds 9100–9119; no data of this study enters them) — a post-hoc transform, no extra training.
- Time-blocked contexts (`rt_blocked.make_blocked_ctx`: whole 100-frame blocks of a run held out, 25-frame buffer removed from training groups, pool and graph; the blocks depend on the seed): seeds 11300–11319 and 11330–11399, 1 draw per budget, learners `baseline, aw8_all, aw8_all_st2lr0.5, lap3_k10, lap3_k10_st4, lap3_k10_st4_shuf, gmc_t_no_rt13`.
Pooled analysis = confirmation 1 ∪ confirmation 2 (70 seeds); confirmation 1 and 2 are also analysed separately.

## Estimand and estimator
Gain of candidate over reference = mean over the cells of a split of [metric(candidate) − metric(reference)] (AUC, accuracy, log-loss with the sign so that positive = candidate better; accuracy / log-loss confidence-weighted, AUC unweighted: exactly the standard endpoint). Resampled estimator (`hi_infer.py`): every held-out judgment is weighted by the multiplicity of its resampling unit in every cell; cells with no positive or no negative judgment after resampling are skipped.

## Inference
- Units: **pair groups (primary)**; sensitivity units: pairs of complete-linkage image clusters at distance 0.03 (`unit_c0.03`, 137 units; also 0.01 and 0.1) and coarse blocks of look-alike groups (`unit_b84`, `unit_b42`, `unit_b21`; the last two are stress tests of strong dependence). Single-linkage clustering chains into one giant component (tau = 0.002: 164 of 168 groups), which is why it is not used.
- Interval: percentile 95 % interval of B = 10 000 bootstrap replicates (resampling the units that occur in the analysed design with replacement), seed 0. Seed-level comparison: Wilcoxon over seeds and bootstrap over seeds on the same cells.
- p-value: exact group-level randomisation test of exchangeability of candidate and reference (random swap of the two learners' predictions for each unit; `Design.perm_contributions`; 20 000 permutations, two-sided); unit = pair group (primary) or `unit_c0.03`.
- Family F1 (Holm): comparisons C1 aw8 vs baseline, C2 aw8_all vs baseline, C2b aw8_all vs aw8, C3 lap3_k10 vs aw8_all, C4 lap3_k10_st4 vs lap3_k10, C5 lap3_k10_st4 vs aw8_all_st2lr0.5 (primary), C6 lap3_k10_st4 vs aw8_all, C7 aw8_all_st2lr0.5 vs aw8_all, C9 gmc_t_no_rt13 vs lap3_k10, C11 gmc_t_no_rt13 vs lap3_k10_st4 × splits {A, B} × metrics {AUC, accuracy}, standard endpoint, all judgments, pooled data: 40 randomisation p-values. Family F2 (Holm over 2): calibration `lap3_k10_st4_cal` vs `lap3_k10_st4`, log-loss, splits A and B (AUC and accuracy secondary). Controls (uncorrected): C8 `lap3_k10_st4` vs `lap3_k10_st4_shuf`. Everything else (strata, HTR, blocked, confirmation 1 vs 2, development replicate, clustered units) is exploratory / sensitivity and labelled so.

## Re-assessment rule (applied to each claim of the paper package's claims table that these runs can test)
Claim (candidate better than reference), pooled standard endpoint, group-level:
- **SUPPORTED**: the AUC interval excludes 0 (lower bound > 0) in both splits AND the accuracy point estimate is positive in both splits AND the Holm-adjusted (F1) randomisation p of the AUC test is < 0.05 in both splits.
- **WEAKER**: not supported, but the AUC point estimate is positive in both splits.
- **UNSUPPORTED**: the AUC point estimate is ≤ 0 in at least one split.
Annotations (do not change the label): "robust to dependence" = the AUC interval also excludes 0 in both splits with `unit_c0.03` units (and, separately, `unit_b42`); "replicated" = the same label in confirmation 1 and confirmation 2 analysed separately; "anchor-near" / "anchor-far" = the same rule within the strata `anc_all_min` ≤ 0.001 and > 0.03 (threshold 0.01 as sensitivity; uncorrected); "time-blocked" = the AUC interval excludes 0 in the blocked contexts (single split, uncorrected). Claims about other quantities (graph dose-response, selection rules, harsh splits, classification endpoint, mechanism observations) cannot be re-assessed from these runs and are reported as "not re-assessed" with the reason.
Calibration claim: supported if the log-loss interval excludes 0 in both splits and the Holm (F2) p < 0.05 in both.

## Descriptive analyses (no decision rule)
Leave-group-out view (per-group gain: mean accuracy / log-loss gain over the group's held-out judgments across cells, per-group AUC contribution; number of groups that gain, sign test) for C3, C4, C5; variance decomposition of the per-judgment gain (log-loss and accuracy; crossed random effects: image-cluster unit, group, seed, cell) and bootstrap standard errors under the different resampling schemes (draws only, seeds only, groups, cluster units, blocks); effective number of independent test units (= iid judgments giving the same standard error); how often every group is in a test set; power analysis: minimal detectable true gain at 80 % power and α = 0.05 (two-sided) = 2.8 × group-bootstrap SE; total number of independent units needed for 80 % power at the observed gain and at half of it = (units in design) × (2.8 SE / gain)², and the implied number of NEW groups beyond the 168.

## Known limits (stated in advance)
Same 168 groups: group-level intervals describe the population of groups that these 168 represent (three growth runs); they do not cover new runs. Groups share images and the images are near-duplicates, so even the group-level interval is optimistic (hence the clustered sensitivities); with only 168 groups the coarse-block bootstrap has very few units. The held-out set of a cell contains 25–40 groups, so the AUC per cell is noisy; the estimator averages over cells. Per-judgment AUC attribution splits each pair's contribution evenly between the two units (exact decomposition, but a convention). The randomisation test assumes exchangeability of the two learners' predictions group by group under the null.
