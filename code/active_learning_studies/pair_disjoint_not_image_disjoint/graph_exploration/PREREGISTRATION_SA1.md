# Pre-registration SA1 (frozen before any confirmation seed) — synthetic anchors, anchor-style fixes and anchor-proximity gating

Written 2026-10-14 (container clock ~12:40 UTC) after development rounds dev1–dev5 and the zero-label diagnostics (seeds 11100–11119; script-generated tables in `SA1_dev_tables.md`). **Confirmation 1 = seeds 11130–11164, confirmation 2 = 11165–11199 (independent, 35 seeds each), splits A and B, one draw per (seed, split, budget), run once each; no confirmation cell exists at the time of this commit.** Run script: `scripts_sa/chain_confirm.sh`; test list: `python3 sa_tables.py confirm SA1_spec.json DIR [BLOCKED_DIR]`.

## What development showed (20 development seeds, optimistic; ≈ 45 learners screened)
1. **Simulator.** No simulator archive was available; a kinematic RHEED renderer (`rheed_sim.py`: reciprocal-lattice rods, exact Ewald/scattering geometry, c(6x2) and RT13 superlattices with domain mixtures, an arbitrary HTR *surrogate*, detector model) was calibrated on real unlabelled frames and real ideal images. After calibration a classifier trained only on synthetic features labels the real (masked) ideal images with 61 % accuracy (31 % before) but a real-vs-synthetic classifier still separates the two perfectly (AUC 1.0); the gap is large (`gap_v*.md`).
2. **Synthetic anchors do not help.** All variants (anchors share 0.1–0.5, types T2/T3/T4, nodes only, anchors + nodes, calibrated bank v1/v2, feature-aligned banks) are null or harmful on accuracy (−0.008…−0.03); the permuted-type control is as good as the correctly typed anchors; a moment-matched Gaussian bank (same per-type mean and covariance, no simulator images) is as good as or better than the aligned simulator bank; augmented real ideal images are no better. In the zero-pair-label diagnostic synthetic anchors alone reach held-out AUC 0.58–0.60 (permuted control 0.41–0.45, real anchors 0.92–0.94).
3. **Real-image preprocessing of the typed anchors matters.** 18 of 154 ideal images (10 HTR, 8 RT13) are 16-bit PNGs saturated by the loader; the (1x1)/c(6x2) ideal images are uncropped (direct beam visible), so they sit far from trajectory frames (median cosine distance 0.69 / 0.31). Masking the rows above the shadow edge (and rescaling the 16-bit files; `sy_mask`) improves the **anchor-far** stratum (AUC +0.027 / +0.026, accuracy ≈ +0.02) and worsens the anchor-near stratum and log-loss; the standard endpoint is flat (AUC ≈ 0, accuracy −0.015 / −0.004). Masking anchors only (not graph nodes) is clearly worse; keeping the clipped 16-bit files weakens the far gain.
4. **Combining the two heads at inference** keeps the near performance and the far gain: the anchor-proximity gate `sy_gate_mask` (baseline head unless both test images are > 0.03 cosine distance from every typed anchor, then the masked-anchor head): AUC +0.0034 / +0.0061, accuracy +0.003 / +0.005, log-loss better, far AUC +0.027 / +0.026; the equal-compute control `sy_gate_base2` (second baseline head with another initialisation) ≈ 0. The mean ensemble `sy_ens_mask`: AUC +0.0025 / +0.0032, accuracy +0.002 / +0.006, far AUC +0.018 / +0.021; control `sy_ens_base2`: −0.0009 / +0.0023 AUC.

## Frozen candidates (3) and controls
Baseline: `lap3_k10_st4` on identical random label sets. Definitions in `nd_synthetic.py` / `syn_specs.py` (`SPEC_OF`).
- **C1 `sy_gate_mask`** = baseline head, but where BOTH test images are farther than 0.03 (cosine distance of centred normalised loader features, the `rt_a1b.py` definition) from every typed anchor, the head trained with direct-beam-masked, rescaled ideal images (`sy_mask`: anchors and graph nodes). Uses only the features of the test images. Control **`sy_gate_base2`** (same gate, second head = baseline architecture with another initialisation: equal compute).
- **C2 `sy_ens_mask`** = mean of the logit gaps of the baseline head and the `sy_mask` head. Control **`sy_ens_base2`** (mean of two baseline heads with different initialisation).
- **C3 `sy_tm_a10`** (the study's synthetic hypothesis) = baseline + synthetic anchors (simulator bank v1 with each type's mean shifted to the real masked ideal-image mean; share 0.10 of the anchor weight, 16 bank images per class per step, all four types). Controls **`sy_tm_a10_perm`** (type labels of the synthetic images permuted; part of the success rule, as the study task requires) and **`sy_tmg_a10`** (Gaussian anchors with the same per-type mean and covariance, no simulator images; reported, not part of the rule).

## Tests and success rule
- **Main family (Holm over 3 candidates × 2 splits × {AUC, accuracy} = 12 tests):** per-seed mean over budgets of (candidate − baseline), two-sided Wilcoxon over the 35 seeds, Holm step-down.
- **Success of a candidate (the study rule):** AUC and accuracy gains positive in both splits (all four > 0), ≥ 3 of the 4 Holm-adjusted p < 0.05, and better than its (first) control (candidate − control > 0 with uncorrected p < 0.05 in ≥ 1 of the four split × metric cells).
- **Far-stratum family (pre-registered secondary; Holm over 3 × 2 × {far AUC, far accuracy} = 12 tests):** both test images > 0.03 from every typed anchor (`rt_a1b.py`; ≈ 15–30 % of the test judgments). Reported next to the main family; it does not change the success rule. **Honesty clauses:** C1 is, by construction, identical to the baseline on the anchor-near stratum, so its far gains are mechanically the gains of the masked head on that stratum; a standard-endpoint gain of C1/C2 is attributed to the far stratum when the near gain is ≈ 0. A far-only gain with a null standard endpoint is reported as such.
- **Time-blocked split (secondary, uncorrected, descriptive):** baseline, C1, C2, C3 on the red-team blocked contexts (35 per confirmation), AUC / accuracy / far / in-block strata / log-loss.
- Other secondaries (not in any rule): log-loss, HTR accuracy, near stratum.
- **Confirmation 2** is an independent repetition of the identical specification (seeds 11165–11199); a candidate is called replicated only if it passes the success rule in both confirmations.

## Expectation stated in advance
C3 is expected to fail (dev: AUC +0.002/+0.003, accuracy −0.001/−0.002 at share 0.10; Gaussian control equal). C1 is expected to be the only candidate with a chance: development AUC gains of +0.003…+0.006 and accuracy +0.003…+0.005 are at the edge of what 35 seeds can resolve (earlier confirmed effects were +0.006…+0.01), the accuracy requirement is the likely failure point, and the development estimates are subject to the winner's curse (≈ 45 learners screened on these seeds).

## Limits
Same 168 labelled pair groups in every seed (re-splits), p-values optimistic; one draw per cell; the simulator is kinematic only (no multiple scattering, no structure factors, HTR surrogate arbitrary); the gate threshold 0.03 equals the far-stratum definition (chosen before the confirmation, not tuned); development used the red-team anchor-distance definition on the loader features of the ideal images (including the 18 saturated ones).
