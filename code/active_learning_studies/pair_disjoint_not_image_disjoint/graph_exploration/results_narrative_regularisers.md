# Is the graph needed? Regularisers on unlabelled images at the long schedule (branch `claude/regularisers-20261013`)

Every table is generated by `rg_report.py` / `rg_tables.py` from the result JSONs (`new_directions/results/rg/*`); the text is hand-written. Same 168 labelled pair groups as all earlier work (re-split with new seeds, **not new data**), so p-values are optimistic; only the pre-registered confirmation (seeds 9050–9084, frozen in `PREREGISTRATION_G2.md` before any of those cells existed, commit d32ccbb3c) counts as evidence; everything on seeds 9000–9011 is development (selected on the same seeds). Test images are near-duplicates of training frames (red-team audit), so every result also has an **anchor-far** version (both test images > 0.03 cosine distance from every typed ideal-image anchor; ≈ 12–15 of ≈ 55–64 test judgments per seed and split).

## 1. Answer
**Pre-registered verdict (rule frozen in advance): B — "the graph adds" — but only through the combined learner, and with a small effect.** Under the frozen rule, the Laplacian + dropout-consistency learner G1 (`rglap3_cons0.3_k10_st4`) beats every graph-free candidate (dropout-consistency, dropout + noise consistency, dropout-consistency with 6× steps): all four differences positive and 4 of 4 Holm-significant against each of them (AUC +0.003…+0.005, accuracy +0.006…+0.012). Its shuffled-graph control is far worse (AUC −0.07/−0.08, accuracy −0.09/−0.10; §4). The plain Laplacian learner `lap3_k10_st4` does **not** meet rule B on its own: it beats the graph-free candidates on accuracy (+0.007…+0.020; Holm-significant in split A, nominal in split B) and the AUC differences are +0.002…+0.004 with confidence intervals that touch zero. **Rule A (graph not needed) failed for all three graph-free candidates**: none is non-inferior within the margins (0.003 AUC / 0.01 accuracy); their AUC is 0.002–0.004 below `lap3_k10_st4` and their accuracy 0.007–0.020 below.

In plain words: a fair search over graph-free regularisers (20 variants, two development rounds) finds learners that recover **most of the AUC gain** of `lap3_k10_st4` over the tuned graph-free baseline (the three graph-free candidates gain +0.010…+0.014 AUC over the tuned baseline vs. +0.015…+0.016 for the graph; development had suggested this), but **not the accuracy gain** (graph-free −0.000…+0.007 over the baseline vs. +0.014…+0.020 for the graph). The graph adds something real on top of any graph-free regulariser I could find, mostly on accuracy; the size is about 0.003 AUC / 0.01–0.02 accuracy. "The graph is needed" holds only in this weak sense; "any regulariser on unlabelled images would do" does not.

**Red-team caveat (anchor-far endpoint, secondary, not in any success rule).** The gains over the tuned graph-free baseline `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` sit in the **anchor-near stratum** for AUC: `lap3_k10_st4` gains +0.019/+0.020 AUC (splits A/B) in the anchor-near stratum but −0.001/+0.009 in the anchor-far stratum (confidence intervals include 0; ≈ 12–15 judgments per seed); the graph-free `rgcons0.3_st4` likewise (+0.015/+0.016 near, −0.004/+0.013 far). On the anchor-far endpoint, no graph-free candidate is distinguishable from `lap3_k10_st4` in either direction (differences −0.006…+0.006 AUC, 95 % intervals about ± 0.01–0.02); the accuracy advantage of the graph over graph-free candidates in split A on the far stratum (+0.020…+0.030) is not Holm-significant. So: **the confirmed graph advantage is demonstrated for the standard endpoint and in the anchor-near stratum; for judgments far from every ideal-image anchor it is unresolved (too few judgments), not shown to be absent.**

## 2. What was added to the protocol
Nothing beyond the earlier graph-learner studies: nodes = all trajectory + ideal images minus the seed's validation/test pair-group images and outer-test ideal images (SHA-256); head = `aw8_all` (frozen SimCLR features, anchor weight 8, all ideal images as anchors); the head is trained in eval mode (its dropout layer is inert in the repository's training loop). New learners (`nd_regularisers.py`, unit-tested to equal `aw8_all` / `cons<L>_st<x>` / `lap3_k10_st<x>` when the new terms are off): dropout / Gaussian-noise / mirror / mixup consistency of unlabelled nodes' scores, R-Drop, hidden-layer dropout, weight decay, EMA, inner-split early stopping, Laplacian on hidden activations, 1-NN ("hard neighbour") Laplacian, and combinations with the Laplacian. Runner `rg_run.py` stores, besides the standard metrics, the anchor-far / anchor-near metrics and the per-judgment score gaps.

## 5. Reading the results
- **Pre-registered and confirmed:** (i) rule A failed (no graph-free candidate non-inferior); (ii) rule B holds for G1; the shuffled-graph controls of both graph learners are far worse (§4), so the graph's *correct neighbourhood structure* matters, not just any smoothness; (iii) `lap3_k10_st4` beats the tuned graph-free baseline in both splits on AUC and accuracy (re-check of the selection-htr confirmation, uncorrected, 35 new seeds: AUC +0.015/+0.016, accuracy +0.020/+0.014).
- **What the data do and do not say about the plain graph:** the graph-free consistency learners are within 0.002–0.004 AUC of `lap3_k10_st4` with intervals that include 0 — so I cannot tell whether the Laplacian is better than dropout-consistency on AUC; the accuracy advantage of the Laplacian (0.007–0.020) is more solid (split A: Holm p < 0.001 against all three; split B: nominal p 0.014–0.065, none Holm-significant).
- **G1 is not better than the plain Laplacian**: G1 − `lap3_k10_st4` is +0.0005/+0.0016 AUC (not significant) and −0.009 (p = 0.007)/−0.001 accuracy (descriptive table in §4.1). G1 passes rule B only because its paired differences against the graph-free candidates have smaller variance than those of the plain Laplacian; the rule was frozen that way, and I report the plain-Laplacian result next to it rather than rounding the verdict up.
- **Anchor-far:** the standard-endpoint advantage of the graph over the tuned baseline is concentrated in anchor-near judgments for AUC (hypothesis: part of the gain comes from smoothing scores between a test frame and its near-duplicate neighbours/anchors, i.e. it exploits that test frames resemble training frames; the far stratum is too small to confirm or refute this). I do not claim any graph or consistency gain for images far from every ideal image.
- **Development observations (not evidence):** the long list of graph-free regularisers that did not work (EMA, weight decay, early stopping, hidden dropout, mixup, mirror, hidden-layer Laplacian, 1-NN Laplacian), and the sensitivity of consistency to weight (3 is worse than 0.3–1).

## 6. Deviations, limits and things I would not over-read
- Schedule: the work ran ≈ 4.7 h; the confirmation ran later than planned because 8 learners × 35 seeds × 2 splits × 4 budgets × 2 draws take ≈ 2 h on 4 cores. The shuffled-graph controls use draw 0 only (half the label sets) — the CTRL tests pair on the common label sets.
- "≤ 10 variants" was interpreted per round (20 in total), the development screens used 12 of the 20 development seeds (9012–9019 unused).
- Non-inferiority at margin 0.003 AUC is demanding with 35 seeds (standard error of the paired AUC gain ≈ 0.0016–0.002): it would have been hard to establish even for a true difference of 0. Rule A failing therefore does **not** show that the graph-free candidates are inferior by more than the margin on AUC; the 95 % intervals of their AUC difference to the Laplacian end at −0.0000…+0.0013, i.e. at or just beyond 0. It does show that they are worse on accuracy by more than the margin in split A.
- The per-seed unit of inference is the seed; the same 168 pair groups and the same test judgments recur across seeds, so confidence intervals are too narrow for the question "would this hold on new data?". The anchor-far numbers rest on ≈ 12–15 judgments per seed.
- Graph-free candidates were tuned on 12 development seeds from a family of consistency losses at one input-dropout rate (0.3) and one noise level; stronger augmentations (real image augmentations in pixel space, as in FixMatch / Mean Teacher with an image encoder) are not testable on frozen features and are left to the GPU list.
- Torch 2.14 CPU numerics reproduce the committed `lap3_k10_st4` / `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` cells of the earlier study to four decimals (seed 8300, split A).
