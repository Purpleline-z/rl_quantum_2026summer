Specification file: `CAL1_spec.json` (candidates, constants, thresholds); code: `new_directions/nd_calibration.py` (stage 1 + calibrators), `cal_run.py` (runner), `cal_tables.py confirm` (tests and verdicts), `cal_figures.py` (reliability diagrams). ## What the development runs showed (observations on 20 seeds; optimistic, selected on the same seeds)
1. The raw `lap3_k10_st4` head is **not uniformly over-confident**: on held-out decisive judgments the test-optimal temperature differs by type — (1x1) T ≈ 1.2–1.4 (soften), c(6x2) ≈ 0.7, HTR ≈ 0.7, **RT13 ≈ 0.3 (sharpen by about 3×: the RT13 scores are compressed)**. A single global temperature is therefore unreliable (log-loss +0.008 / −0.002 in split A, −0.022 / −0.037 in split B for dev1 / dev2).
2. Per-type temperatures help: fitted only on the labelled set by 5-fold (by pair group) out-of-fold logits with a ridge to T = 1 (`T_type0_tau2`): log-loss +0.018 / +0.013, ECE +0.010…+0.012, AUC +0.003…+0.005, accuracy unchanged; centred on constants fitted on development runs (`T_typeprior_tau8`): log-loss +0.033…+0.040, ECE +0.016…+0.020, AUC +0.009…+0.010. The cross-fit logit ensemble (final head + the 5 fold heads averaged, `CF`) alone: log-loss +0.002…+0.009, AUC +0.002…+0.005, accuracy +0.003…+0.005, ECE not better. Ensemble + prior temperatures (`CF_typeprior_tau8`): log-loss +0.036…+0.045.
3. Not helpful (development-only, not frozen): label smoothing 0.1 / 0.25 in the Bradley–Terry loss (worse log-loss and ECE, with or without a following temperature), a Davidson ties model fitted post hoc on all outcomes, distance-to-anchor / distance-to-labelled-image / graph-density-weighted temperatures, shrinking far-from-anchor scores, a 5-head full-data ensemble (about the same as the cross-fit ensemble; not frozen for cost).
4. Per-type constants fitted on pooled development out-of-fold logits of the labelled judgments (single-head logits, no test label): (1x1) 1.352, c(6x2) 0.682, RT13 0.293, HTR 0.630; stable across splits and budgets (pooled test-optimal temperatures: 1.20, 0.71, 0.34, 0.68). Leave-one-seed-out versions of the priors were used in all development comparisons above.

## Frozen method definitions
Control **`raw`** = the registry learner `lap3_k10_st4` (aw8_all + Laplacian λ = 3 on the k = 10 cosine kNN graph of unlabelled images + 4× training steps), unchanged. All candidates start from the **same label set and the same final head** (head seed `7·seed + draw`; verified equal to the registry learner in the unit tests).
Cross-fitting: the labelled judgments are split into 5 folds **by pair group** (fold assignment seeded by `7·seed + draw`); 5 fold heads are trained on the other 4 folds with the same learning rate and number of steps as the full head (not the smaller set's schedule); out-of-fold (OOF) logits of every labelled judgment come from the head that did not see its group. Calibrators read only the OOF logits and weights of the labelled **decisive** judgments (ties and not_apply are ignored) and image-geometry features; **no test label, no validation group of split A and no label outside the budget** is used (unit test: all variants are invariant to the test labels).
Grid for a log temperature: 161 points on [ln 0.2, ln 5].
- **A1 `T_type0_tau2` (within-run, per type)**: for every type k, ln T_k = argmin over the grid of Σ_i w_i·logloss_i(d_i / T_k) over the OOF decisive judgments of type k + 2·(ln T_k)² (ridge towards T = 1; types without labelled judgments keep T = 1). Applied as d / T_k.
- **A2 `CF` (cross-fit ensemble, no temperature)**: mean of the logits of the final head and the 5 fold heads.
- **A3 `T_typeprior_tau8` (per type, development prior)**: as A1 but the ridge is centred on the development constants (1x1 1.352, c(6x2) 0.682, RT13 0.293, HTR 0.630) with weight 8: ln T_k = argmin Σ w_i logloss_i(d_i / T_k) + 8·(ln T_k − ln c_k)².
- **A4 `CF_typeprior_tau8`**: the A3 temperatures (fitted on the single-head OOF logits) applied to the A2 ensemble logits.
Constants of A3/A4 come from development seeds (as λ = 3 and the schedule ×4 did for the learner): they are fitted on labelled-judgment OOF logits of development runs, never on the confirmation seeds; they are, like every choice made on these 168 pair groups, not independent of the confirmation data (same groups, re-split).

## Endpoint and tests
Same preference endpoint as before (held-out decisive judgments of image-disjoint test groups; confidence-weighted): **log-loss** and **ECE**, plus AUC and accuracy as safety metrics. **ECE** = 10 equal-width bins on the confidence of the predicted winner (0.5–1), confidence-weighted Σ|accuracy − mean confidence| over bins, **pooled over all of a seed's label sets (budgets 10/20/40/60 × 2 draws) per split** (a single 60-judgment test set gives a too noisy ECE). Label sets: budgets 10/20/40/60 judgments × draws 0 and 1 (`random_labelled`), identical for all variants. Per seed and split, each metric is averaged over budgets and draws (ECE pooled as stated); per candidate and split the paired difference to `raw` (log-loss, ECE: raw − candidate, so positive = better) is tested with a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test across the 35 seeds.
**Family** = 4 candidates × 2 splits × {log-loss, ECE} = 16 tests, Holm-corrected over all 16.
**Success of a candidate** (user's rule): log-loss gain positive in both splits; at least 3 of its 4 tests (2 splits × {log-loss, ECE}) positive and Holm p < 0.05; and neither safety metric worse than the margin in either split (mean paired AUC difference ≥ −0.002, accuracy ≥ −0.005).
Expectation stated in advance: A1, A3, A4 pass log-loss; ECE (a noisy statistic) may keep 1–2 candidates below the "3 of 4" line; A2 alone is expected to change log-loss little and not ECE.

## Secondary, descriptive (not in any success rule; uncorrected)
(a) **Anchor-far endpoint** (red-team stratum): test judgments with both images > 0.03 (1 − cos, frozen SimCLR features, centred and normalised) from every typed ideal-image anchor (`anc_min > 0.03`), vs the anchor-near stratum: log-loss and accuracy gains per candidate; any gain that exists only in the anchor-near stratum is stated as such. (b) Per-type log-loss and accuracy, per-budget gains, reliability diagrams (`cal_figures.py`), ECE per type. (c) **Calibrated comparison with the tuned graph-free learner `aw8_all_st2lr0.5`** (one draw per budget, same 5-fold cross-fitting): both learners receive the same calibrators; paired differences of log-loss/ECE/AUC/accuracy overall, by stratum and by type — answers whether the log-loss deficit of `lap3_k10_st4` reported by the red team (RT13, anchor-far) is a scale effect. (d) The uncalibrated variants of the development list (global temperature, shrunk-to-global per-type, distance-weighted, Davidson) are recomputed from the same stage-1 files and shown as exploratory; the label-smoothing and 5-head-ensemble variants are not run on the confirmation seeds.
Development-only side runs (seeds 9100–9119, one draw, not part of the confirmation): calibrated comparison with `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` and `lap3_k10`; a learner-side check that switches the Laplacian penalty off for the RT13 score (`lap3_k10_st4_rt13off`). (Cross-fitting with other K, and a fold graph without the held-out fold's images — one label set: OOF logit scale changes by < 1 % — were not pursued before the freeze.)

The confirmation is run once; nothing is re-tuned after seeing it; if a cell fails it is re-run with identical code. Limits: same 168 pair groups re-split (p-values optimistic; 35 seeds are not 35 independent data sets); 28–70 decisive test judgments per seed; only 5–13 decisive labelled judgments per type are available to a within-run calibrator, which is why A1 is ridged and A3/A4 are centred on a development prior; the raw head is evaluated in its own logit scale (no calibration of the *ranking*, only of the scale and the per-type offset in scale).
