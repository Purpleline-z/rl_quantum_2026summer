# PREREGISTRATION_S2 — replication of the two graph-coverage rules on fresh seeds (written 2026-10-12 ~10:25 UTC, AFTER confirmation blocks 1–3 were read, BEFORE any seed 8340–8399 was run)
Why: in S1 the typed-coverage rules were the only graph rules whose gain over their type-only control was supported, but the pre-registered rule (accuracy AND AUC Holm-significant in both splits) was missed narrowly (regular start: qcov_alloc 3 of 4 cells, tdc 2 of 4; cold start: AUC cells significant, accuracy not). S2 repeats exactly those two rules on seeds not used before. It is a second look at the same hypothesis chosen after seeing S1, so S2 is NOT independent of the selection of the hypothesis; its role is to check that the S1 pattern is not a fluke of the seeds.
Rules (unchanged code, same as S1): C3 `tdc`, C4 `qcov_alloc`; comparator arms `tdc_typeonly`, `tdc_shuffled`, `qcov_alloc_typeonly`, `qcov_alloc_shuffled`; Random = 5 draws. Learner aw8_all, protocol unchanged.
Blocks (each run once): cold start, seeds 8340–8369 (30); regular start, seeds 8370–8399 (30).
Family per block: {tdc, qcov_alloc} x {A, B} x {acc, auc} = 8 tests (two-sided Wilcoxon over seeds, gain = per-seed mean over budgets vs Random), Holm within the block. A rule 'beats Random' in a block if all four of its cells are Holm < 0.05. Graph-specificity (descriptive, uncorrected): paired differences to the type-only and shuffled controls in both splits.
Pooled descriptive estimate (not a test): S1 + S2 seeds of the same start mode (65 seeds per start mode), mean gain with a seed bootstrap interval, reported next to the S1-only and S2-only numbers.
