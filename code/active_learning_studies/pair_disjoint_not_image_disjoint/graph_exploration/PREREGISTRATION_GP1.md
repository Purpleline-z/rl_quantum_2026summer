# Pre-registration GP1 (frozen before any confirmation seed) — graph-kernel Gaussian process vs `lap3_k10_st4`

Written 2026-10-14 (container clock UTC; see the commit time of this file) after the development screens on seeds 11000–11019. **Confirmation 1 = seeds 11030–11064, confirmation 2 = 11065–11099** (35 each, splits A and B, plus the time-blocked split; each run once, nothing re-tuned between them). No confirmation cell exists at the time of this commit. Specification files: `GP1_spec.json` (candidates, constants, thresholds), `GP1_blocked_spec.json`; code: `new_directions/nd_gpg.py` (model), `gp_run.py` (stage-1 runner), `gp_tables.py` (stage-2 tests and verdicts), `scripts_gp/run_conf.sh`. Seeds registered in `nd_core.py` (`GP_*`).

## The model (one paragraph)
One latent utility f_k per reconstruction type on the frozen SimCLR features (cosine geometry of the mean-centred, L2-normalised features). Prior f_k ~ GP(0, a_k k~) with the **semi-supervised deformed kernel** k~(x,y) = k(x,y) − k_x^T (I + βLK)^-1 βL k_y on the cosine-kNN graph (k = 10) of the identity-safe unlabelled image nodes of `nd_graph_learner.GraphData` (held-out and outer-test images are never nodes; they enter through k only) — the exact GP version of "RKHS penalty + β f^T L f", i.e. of the Laplacian learner's smoothness prior (verified numerically: k~ = (K^-1 + βL)^-1 on the nodes). Likelihood as the repository's loss, per type: decisive pair → Bradley–Terry with the confidence weight; tie → sqrt(Δ² + 1e-4); not_apply → softplus(f) on both images; typed ideal images (references + utility + outer-test ideal images, the `aw8_all` anchors) as ranking constraints with the head's weights (aw / 12 per class pair, mean over image pairs, times the number of labelled rows); bad images softplus(f + 1). Inference: Laplace (Newton in the dual), 0.1–10 s per fit on one CPU thread; predictive mean and Laplace variance of f(a) − f(b). Base kernel RBF with length ℓ = c × median pairwise distance of the nodes.

## What the development screens showed (seeds 11000–11019; selected on the same seeds, optimistic)
1. Dev1 (draw 0), dev2 (draw 1), dev4 (draws 1–2): the best fixed GP is ahead of `lap3_k10_st4` in AUC by ≈ +0.010 (A) / +0.005 (B) with accuracy ≈ +0.009 / +0.001 and about equal raw log-loss; calibrated `lap3_k10_st4` (CAL1 per-type constants) is better in log-loss than the GP's native probabilities (the GP is under-confident for RT13 / c(6x2) / HTR exactly as the MLP is, needs temperatures 0.4–0.9; amplitude and likelihood sharpness are one parameter in a GP, so "calibration for free" fails); calibrated GP vs calibrated lap3: log-loss +0.012 / +0.001, AUC +0.006 / +0.002.
2. **Graph:** kernel without the graph deformation (β = 0) is within 0.003–0.005 AUC of the graph version in the standard splits and equal or better in the time-blocked split; a shuffled graph destroys the GP (AUC −0.09, accuracy −0.11): the graph helps only if right, adds ≈ +0.003–0.005 AUC over the plain kernel.
3. **Hyperparameter learning:** Laplace evidence (all terms or conditional on the anchors) chooses β = 0 and longer length scales and loses 0.005–0.02 AUC; per-type CV with an amplitude prior (`gp_cvt_p` / `gp_cvt3`) is about equal to `lap3_k10_st4` in AUC (−0.001 … +0.009) and 0.001–0.008 lower in accuracy; no learned selection reached the dev-tuned fixed setting (the length scale chosen by likelihood criteria is longer than the AUC-optimal one).
4. **Variance:** the predictive sd of f(a) − f(b) rises with the distance to the nearest anchor (Spearman +0.2 … +0.4) but is not informative about errors (AUROC 0.30 for "sd predicts an error": sd grows with the score margin); the variance-aware (probit) score never improves log-loss in any stratum (γ-scan: ≤ 0.002).
5. **Time-blocked split (dev, 20 seeds):** GP minus `lap3_k10_st4`: AUC +0.026, accuracy +0.027, log-loss +0.027, anchor-far AUC +0.024 (all p ≤ 0.002); `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` is worse than `lap3_k10_st4` (AUC −0.016). Cold start (16 of 20 seeds, partial): AUC +0.013 … +0.018, accuracy +0.011 / −0.006.
6. Not helpful (dev): arc-cosine kernel (the head's own NNGP function class; AUC 0.935), RBF mixtures, graph-diffusion kernel mixture, graph-propagated type-posterior mean function (monotonically worse with its weight), larger or smaller anchor weight (flat), averaging the calibrated GP and calibrated lap3 logits (≤ +0.004 AUC).
All variants/settings tried are listed in `PROGRESS_LOG_graph_gp.md`; the hyperparameter screens were grids of one variant (≈ 150 fixed settings on the dev seeds), declared as hand-tuning of the hand-set variant.

## Frozen candidates and controls
Reference **`lap3_k10_st4`** (registry learner, raw) and **calibrated reference `lap3_k10_st4+Tcal`** = its logits divided by the per-type constants of the calibration study (1x1 1.352, c(6x2) 0.682, RT13 0.293, HTR 0.630; `T_typeprior_fixed`, the better of the two confirmed calibrations, no test or in-budget fit). Same label sets, same random draws for every learner (`random_labelled`, head seed 7·seed + draw).
- **C1 `gp_fix3`** (hand-set): RBF, ℓ = 0.25 × median, β = 0.1, per-type amplitudes (1x1, c(6x2), RT13, HTR) = (0.5, 1, 1, 1), anchor weight aw = 32.
- **C2 `gp_fix3|mean+Tfix`** = C1 with per-type temperatures from development constants (1x1 0.886, c(6x2) 0.515, RT13 0.358, HTR 0.421; fitted on the out-of-fold logits of the labelled judgments of development runs; no test label).
- **C3 `gp_cvt3`** (learned in budget): per type, kernel shape (ℓ ∈ {0.15, 0.25, 0.4} × median, β ∈ {0.03, 0.1, 0.3}) and amplitude ∈ {0.3, 1, 3} chosen by grouped (by pair group) 3-fold cross-validated decisive log-loss of the in-budget judgments, with a ridge 0.05·(ln a − ln a₀)² towards the C1 pattern a₀ = (0.5, 1, 1, 1); aw = 32.
- **C4 `gp_cvt3|mean+Tprior`** = C3 with within-run per-type temperatures: the CAL1 calibrator `fit_type_t(OOF logits, τ = 8, centre = development constants (1x1 0.801, c(6x2) 0.581, RT13 0.388, HTR 0.331))`, out-of-fold logits from 5 group folds of the in-budget judgments.
- Controls (not candidates): `gp_fix3_noG` (β = 0: kernel without graph), `gp_fix3_shuf` (node identities of the graph shuffled, degree sequence kept), `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` (tuned graph-free MLP head).
Scores used: the predictive MEAN of f(a) − f(b) (`|mean`); the variance-aware probit score is secondary. The development constants of C2/C4, the hand-set values of C1 and the amplitude prior of C3 come from development seeds (as λ = 3 and the ×4 schedule did for the learner); they are not independent of the 168 pair groups.

## Endpoint and tests
Standard endpoint (held-out decisive judgments of image-disjoint test groups, confidence-weighted accuracy / log-loss, pooled AUC), splits A and B, budgets 10/20/40/60 judgments × draws 0 and 1; per seed and split each metric is averaged over budgets and draws; paired differences (log-loss: reference − candidate) are tested with two-sided Wilcoxon over the 35 seeds.
**Primary family (Holm over 12 tests):** C1 and C3 vs `lap3_k10_st4` on AUC and accuracy × splits A, B (8 tests); C2 and C4 vs `lap3_k10_st4+Tcal` on log-loss × splits A, B (4 tests).
**Success of a raw candidate (C1 or C3), per the task's rule — either branch:**
 (A) AUC and accuracy gains positive in both splits and ≥ 3 of these 4 tests Holm p < 0.05; or
 (B) non-inferior to `lap3_k10_st4` — the lower end of the 95 % paired-bootstrap interval of the seed-level difference ≥ −0.003 AUC and ≥ −0.01 accuracy in both splits — **and** its calibrated twin (C2 for C1, C4 for C3) beats `lap3_k10_st4+Tcal` in log-loss with Holm p < 0.05 and positive gain in both splits.
**Expectation stated in advance:** branch A is not expected (accuracy gain ≈ 0, AUC gain significant at most in split A); branch B is expected to fail at the log-loss condition in split B (development gain +0.001). C3 is expected to be about equal to the reference. A not-met verdict for both is the likely outcome.
**Secondary families (descriptive unless stated):** (i) the time-blocked split (rt_blocked contexts: whole 100-frame blocks of a run held out with a 25-frame buffer removed from labelled data, pool and graph; one context per seed; 35 seeds; draws 0, 1): C1 and C3 vs `lap3_k10_st4` on AUC and accuracy, Holm over these 4 tests, pre-registered as a separate family (`GP1_blocked_spec.json`); (ii) the anchor-far stratum (both test images > 0.03 from every typed anchor) AUC and log-loss of every candidate vs both references; (iii) controls: C1 vs `gp_fix3_noG` and `gp_fix3_shuf` on AUC / accuracy / log-loss (uncorrected; "graph claim" met if C1 beats the shuffled graph); (iv) cold start (10 random initial judgments; confirmation 1 seeds, one draw); (v) predictive-variance diagnostics (sd by anchor distance, AUROC of sd for errors) and timing per fit; (vi) the probit score; (vii) exploratory combinations computed from the stored logits (e.g. mean of C2 and the calibrated reference) are labelled exploratory.
