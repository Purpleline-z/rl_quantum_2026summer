# PREREGISTRATION_GE1 — frozen candidates, families and success rules (graph-endpoint study; written 2026-10-13, BEFORE any seed >= 8500 was run)
Dev evidence behind the choices: `PREREGISTRATION_GE0.md` and the dev screens (seeds 8400–8419, `results/ge/dev_*`); dev numbers carry a winner's curse and are not evidence. Branch `claude/graph-endpoint-1013`.

## Blocks (each run once)
Confirmation 1 = seeds 8500–8534 (35), the block on which the success rules below are judged. Confirmation 2 = seeds 8550–8584 (35), an independent replication of exactly the same analysis, reported separately and never pooled with block 1. A claim is called 'confirmed' only if block 1 satisfies its rule; it is called 'robustly confirmed' only if block 2 also does; otherwise 'not confirmed' / 'not robustly confirmed'.

## Protocol extensions (all marked; see GE0 for details)
E1 classification endpoint on the paper split (28 outer-test ideal images; labelled = 88 references only), with INDUCTIVE and TRANSDUCTIVE graph protocols reported in separate tables (transductive = looser; its conclusions are never merged with inductive ones). E2 dose-response of the number of unlabelled images in the graph. E3 harsh preference splits (held-out growth run, held-out top temperature tercile; the ordinary split is already image-disjoint). Preference runs of E2/E3 use budgets 10/20/40/60 with 2 random label sets per budget (earlier studies used 3), splits A and B for E2; gain per seed = mean over budgets and draws. The harsh test sets are deterministic (3 runs / 1 tercile): seeds vary the initial groups, the labelled draws and training randomness, NOT the test judgments (about 50 decisive judgments for run, 26 for temperature). Seed-level Wilcoxon p-values therefore describe training-set variability only; a test-group bootstrap interval is reported next to every harsh result as a caveat but is not part of the criterion.

## Candidates (4 methods) and comparators
Candidates: `lap3_k10` (Laplacian-regularised `aw8_all` head, lambda 3, cosine kNN k 10 on SimCLR features; the frozen learner of PREREGISTRATION_G1), `laplr` (Laplacian-regularised logistic regression: C from the references' 5-fold CV, manifold weight gamma in {0, .01, .1, 1} chosen by the same inner CV on the references), `sgc` (logistic regression on one-step propagated features), `lap3_ref` (the lap3_k10 head with reference-only anchors, scored by the win-rate rule of the paper).
Comparators / controls: classification — `nn` (cosine 1-NN, primary reference; reproduces the baseline branch's paper-seed numbers exactly), `logreg`, `aw8_ref` (non-graph head with reference-only anchors), shuffled-graph controls `laplr_shuf`, `lap3_ref_shuf`; preference — `aw8_all`, `lap3_k10_shuf` (node identities permuted), `lap3_k10_n3` (graph built on heavily noised features).
NOT carried to confirmation (dev-only, reported as such): label spreading (`ls`, also on an ideal-image-only graph), 2-layer GCN classifier, `fusion`, SGC on ideal-only graph, lambda selected by internal validation (`lap_auto`: dev result — it picked lambda 1 in 68% of fits and was not better than fixed lambda 3), `lap1`/`lap10` as separate candidates.

## Families, tests, success rules
Test = two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank over the 35 seeds (zero differences dropped); Holm within the family; paired differences to controls are uncorrected.
(a) CLASSIFICATION, per block: family K1 = {laplr-inductive, laplr-transductive, sgc-inductive, sgc-transductive, lap3_ref-inductive at 60 judgments} minus `nn`, accuracy on the 28 test images, 5 tests, Holm. Success (a): at least one of them has a positive difference and Holm < 0.05; the inductive and transductive members are read separately. Secondary K2 = the same five minus `logreg` (Holm over 5). Controls (uncorrected): `lap3_ref` − `aw8_ref`, `lap3_ref` − `lap3_ref_shuf`, `laplr` − `laplr_shuf`.
(b) DOSE-RESPONSE, per block: gain g(p) = (`lap3_k10` with graph nodes = ideal images + a fixed per-seed random fraction p of the trajectory frames) − `aw8_all`, AUC and accuracy, p in {0, .25, .5, 1}. Family D = {split A, split B} x {AUC, accuracy} = 4 tests; each test = Wilcoxon over seeds of the per-seed least-squares slope of g on p. Success (b): slope positive and Holm < 0.05 in at least 3 of the 4 tests. Mandatory descriptive companions: g at every level, the paired difference g(1) − g(.25) (uncorrected; a plateau/decline is reported as such even if the trend test passes), the series 'out' (graph = ideal images + all pair-group images + fraction p of the frames outside every pair group), the extra level p = .1, and the shuffled-graph control at p = 1.
(c) HARSH SPLITS, per block: family H = {held-out run, held-out temperature tercile} x {AUC, accuracy}, each `lap3_k10` − `aw8_all`, 4 tests, Holm. Success (c): all four differences positive, at least 3 with Holm < 0.05, AND `lap3_k10` beats `lap3_k10_shuf` (uncorrected p < 0.05 for AUC or accuracy, in both split kinds). Companions: `lap3_k10` − `lap3_k10_n3`; the standard-split gain on the same seeds (the p = 1 variant of the dose block); test-group bootstrap intervals.
Everything not listed is exploratory and will be labelled so.

## Known limitations written in advance
All seeds re-split the same 168 pair groups / 150 ideal images, so p-values are optimistic. The 28-image classification test has binomial noise of about 0.06 per seed. The harsh split has 3 growth runs only. Dev-stage observations that led to these choices: graph classifiers did not beat 1-NN or logistic regression on dev seeds (best graph method about equal to logistic regression, shuffled graph equal); the dose-response on dev seeds was a step then plateau/decline, not monotone; lap3_k10 kept a gain of about +0.02 AUC on the harsh splits. The confirmation runs may contradict all of this.
