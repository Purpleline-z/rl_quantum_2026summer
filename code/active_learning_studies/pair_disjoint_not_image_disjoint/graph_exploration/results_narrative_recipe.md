## 2. What was done
- **Learner code.** `new_directions/nd_recipe.py`: one fit routine with a switch for every knob; with default settings it reproduces `lap3_k10_st4` to the last digit (unit test), and the switches are tested individually (`code_behavior_tests/test_recipe.py`, `test_rc_tables.py`).
- **Protocol (unchanged).** judgment-unit protocol, splits A and B, budgets 10/20/40/60 judgments, identical random label sets for all learners, held-out decisive preference judgments (about 56–70 per seed and split). Development: 2 random label sets per budget; confirmation: 3.
- **Anchor-far endpoint (red-team caveat).** The labelled data are 284 frames of three growth runs and most held-out images are near-duplicates of training or anchor images. Besides the standard endpoint every table reports an *anchor-far* endpoint: both test images more than 0.03 (1 − cos, centred/normalised SimCLR features) from every typed ideal-image anchor (`anc_all_min` of the red team's `rt_a1b.py`). This keeps only ≈ 15 % of the judgments (4–15 per cell), so it has little power (table `dev_mde`); "anchor-near" is ≤ 0.001. Per-stratum numbers are descriptive.
- **Development plan and amendment** (`PREREGISTRATION_RC0_development_plan.md`): 12 single-knob variants + 1 combination; written before any result was read; the amendment RC0b (rank-based shortlisting, because the promotion rule would have promoted nothing) was written after seeing 8 of the 10 tier-1 seeds.
