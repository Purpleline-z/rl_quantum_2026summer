# Recipe search around `lap3_k10_st4` (branch `claude/recipe-20261013`)

Every table is generated by `rc_report.py` / `rc_tables.py` / `rc_confirm.py` from the result JSONs (`new_directions/results/rc/*`); the text around them is hand-written. Same 168 labelled pair groups as all earlier work (re-split with new seeds, **not new data**): p-values are optimistic; only the pre-registered confirmation (seeds 9350–9384, frozen in `PREREGISTRATION_RC1.md` before any of those cells existed) counts as evidence; development seeds are 9300–9319. Incumbent = `lap3_k10_st4` = `aw8_all` + Laplacian regulariser (λ = 3, k = 10 cosine-kNN graph of unlabelled images) + 4× training steps; it is the unchanged reference of every comparison here.

## 1. Answer
**Pre-registered confirmation (seeds 9350–9384, 35 seeds, splits A and B, run once; section 4): no recipe change beats the unchanged incumbent `lap3_k10_st4`.** Of the four frozen candidates (cosine learning-rate decay `rc_cos`; per-type learning rate for RT13/HTR `rc_ptlr`; anchor weight 16 `rc_aw16`; cosine decay + weight decay 1.0), 0 of 4 meet the success rule (all four AUC/accuracy gains positive and ≥ 3 of 4 Holm-significant; Holm over 16 tests). Only 2 of the 16 main tests are Holm-significant — the AUC gain of `rc_cos` and of the combination in split A (+0.0017 each) — and in both cases split B is not significant and the accuracy gains are ≈ 0 (−0.0029 … +0.0013 over all candidates). That is the size of the effect 35 seeds can just resolve (minimum detectable ≈ +0.003 AUC at the Holm level, table `dev_mde`), i.e. nothing of practical size. HTR (separate family): 0 of 4 succeed; `rc_ptlr` improves HTR log-loss (+0.005 / +0.008, Holm-significant in both splits) but not HTR accuracy (+0.001 / +0.002, n.s.); `rc_aw16` also improves HTR log-loss (+0.009) but loses HTR accuracy (−0.008 / −0.003); cosine decay and the combination lose HTR log-loss. **Anchor-far endpoint** (both test images > 0.03 from every typed anchor; ≈ 15 % of the judgments, low power): no candidate shows a significant gain; `rc_aw16` is lower on far accuracy (−0.010 / −0.013, Holm-n.s.). So there is no gain that exists "only in the anchor-near stratum" among the candidates — there is no gain.

**Development (seeds 9300–9319; observations, optimistic, not evidence):** 12 single-knob variants and 1 combination were screened; nothing improved on the incumbent. All AUC/accuracy changes lie within about ±0.005 except two clearly harmful ones (dropout 0.3 active in training, about −0.10 to −0.13 AUC; hinge anchor loss, −0.017/−0.004 accuracy and −0.05/−0.04 log-loss). Several variants improve log-loss a little (deeper head, anchor weight 16, Rao–Kupper, full-graph Laplacian, +0.003 … +0.011) without improving AUC or accuracy. The recipe therefore sits on a plateau for the knobs screened; the incumbent is not an accident of a lucky corner for these settings. Seven further knobs were not screened (12-variant budget; listed in 2.1).

**Context (replication, not part of the families):** on these new seeds the incumbent again beats the tuned graph-free control `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` (AUC +0.016 / +0.018, accuracy +0.021 / +0.024, A / B, all p < 1e-5). Following the red-team caveat, where that gain lives: AUC +0.032 / +0.031 in the anchor-near stratum (≤ 0.001) but only +0.011 / +0.015 in the anchor-far stratum (intervals include / touch 0), accuracy +0.033 / +0.040 in the anchor-far stratum (significant) and −0.014 / −0.012 in the 0.001–0.03 band (section 4). So the learner's AUC advantage is concentrated near the typed anchors, in line with the red-team audit; its accuracy advantage is not confined to them.

## 2. What was done
- **Learner code.** `new_directions/nd_recipe.py`: one fit routine with a switch for every knob; with default settings it reproduces `lap3_k10_st4` to the last digit (unit test), and the switches are tested individually (`code_behavior_tests/test_recipe.py`, `test_rc_tables.py`).
- **Protocol (unchanged).** judgment-unit protocol, splits A and B, budgets 10/20/40/60 judgments, identical random label sets for all learners, held-out decisive preference judgments (about 56–70 per seed and split). Development: 2 random label sets per budget; confirmation: 3.
- **Anchor-far endpoint (red-team caveat).** The labelled data are 284 frames of three growth runs and most held-out images are near-duplicates of training or anchor images. Besides the standard endpoint every table reports an *anchor-far* endpoint: both test images more than 0.03 (1 − cos, centred/normalised SimCLR features) from every typed ideal-image anchor (`anc_all_min` of the red team's `rt_a1b.py`). This keeps only ≈ 15 % of the judgments (4–15 per cell), so it has little power (table `dev_mde`); "anchor-near" is ≤ 0.001. Per-stratum numbers are descriptive.
- **Development plan and amendment** (`PREREGISTRATION_RC0_development_plan.md`): 12 single-knob variants + 1 combination; written before any result was read; the amendment RC0b (rank-based shortlisting, because the promotion rule would have promoted nothing) was written after seeing 8 of the 10 tier-1 seeds.

### 3.8 Development observations
- **Plateau.** No variant improves AUC or accuracy over the incumbent in both splits; the best 20-seed ranking score is +0.0001 (cosine decay). Between-seed SD of the paired gain is ≈ 0.005 AUC / 0.008 accuracy.
- **Harmful:** dropout 0.3 during training (scores shrink, AUC −0.13 / −0.10; an implementation check — dropout 1e-9 reproduces the incumbent exactly, 0.05 / 0.1 / 0.3 give AUC 0.945 / 0.946 / 0.877 vs 0.948 on a test cell with accuracy 0.884 / 0.874 / 0.744 vs 0.913 — confirms the effect is real, not a bug), hinge anchors (log-loss and accuracy), width 1024 (slightly negative and twice as slow).
- **Log-loss only:** depth 2, anchor weight 16, Rao–Kupper, full-graph Laplacian, per-type learning rate improve log-loss by 0.002–0.011 but not AUC or accuracy (calibration, not ranking).
- **Per-type:** no variant changes one type consistently in both splits and both metrics (table 3.4); the largest tier-1 type effects are the harmful variants'.
- **Per-budget:** no budget-dependent pattern beyond noise (table 3.5).
- **Overlap with the parallel `regularisers` branch** (graph-free regularisers on unlabelled images at the long schedule): hidden dropout 0.5 and weight decay 1.0 were also clearly or mildly worse there; the implementations differ (their weight decay and dropout settings are not identical to `rc_wd1`, `rc_do0.3`), so the numbers are not directly comparable, and mixup there is a consistency term on unlabelled nodes whereas `rc_mix` is pair-level mixup of labelled rows (neutral to slightly negative here).
- **Process notes (honest accounting).** The promotion rule written in RC0 would have promoted nothing; the rank-based amendment RC0b was written after seeing 8 of the 10 tier-1 seeds (the rule is generic, but it was not written blind). The two unused slots went to `rc_softtie` and `rc_ptlr`. The frozen candidates were chosen by rank among all screened learners, so the confirmation is a direct winner's-curse test; it came out null.

## 5. Limits
- Same 168 labelled groups and 284 frames of three growth runs as all earlier work; seeds only re-split them, so seed-level p-values are optimistic and nothing here speaks to new runs or samples. Test sets are ≈ 56–70 decisive judgments per (seed, split); the red-team audit showed most test images are near-duplicates of training or anchor frames.
- The anchor-far stratum keeps ≈ 15 % of the judgments (4–15 per cell); its AUC is undefined for single-class cells; power is low (MDE ≈ +0.013 AUC, +0.019 accuracy at 35 seeds), so "no far gain" means "none detectable".
- One-at-a-time screening around one operating point (λ = 3, k = 10, 400 steps, lr 1e-3/3e-3); interactions beyond the one combination, other λ/k/step counts (studied by other branches), and seven knobs (anchor weight 4, widths 128/512, depth 3, down-weighting tie/not_apply rows, AdamW betas, gradient clipping off) were not examined; confidence-weight power is a no-op for this data.
- Development used 2 random label sets per budget (confirmation 3) and the tier-1 screen used only 10 seeds, so small real effects could have been missed in tier 1; the 20-seed extension covered only the four top-ranked variants and the combination.
- The shortlisting amendment RC0b was written after part of tier 1 had been seen (see 3.8).
