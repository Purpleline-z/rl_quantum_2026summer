# Literature notes — score propagation (branch `claude/score-propagation-20261014`)

Only what the search snippets said is recorded ("(snippet)" = not read in full); nothing here was run on this data.

- **Graph-Laplacian ranking with a Bradley–Terry likelihood.** The search found no single paper with exactly "Laplacian-regularised Bradley–Terry"; the closest are the AAAI-19 "Pref-Rank" paper (ranking the nodes of a known graph from a subset of pairwise preferences, BTL model + Laplacian-based kernel, generalisation bounds) and Szummer & Yilmaz (CIKM 2011, semi-supervised learning to rank with preference regularisation) (snippets); Ando & Zhang, "Learning on graph with Laplacian regularization" (NIPS 2006) is the transductive-bound reference (snippet). Maximum-likelihood BT is a convex problem (snippet) — the free-score model here is that problem plus a graph penalty.
- **Pure-Laplacian failure mode.** In the very-few-labels limit Laplacian penalties can produce spikes at labelled points (p-Laplacian literature, from the neighbouring recipe/regularisers searches) — relevant to the free-score model, which has no parametric smoothness except the graph.
- **Distilling a propagation teacher into a parametric student.** GKD (Springer 2021) uses label propagation in the teacher and a graph-independent student; CPF (arXiv 2103.02885) uses a blend of parameterised label propagation and a feature transformation as student; GLNN / "Propagate & Distill" (arXiv 2311.17781) distil GNN outputs into MLPs, the latter after propagating the teacher's outputs (snippets). → hybrid (b) here distils the pair differences of free-score node scores into the Laplacian head.
- **Stacking / local residual correction.** The search found no source for "parametric model + cross-validated stacking weight + kernel residual correction" as one method; background only (Nadaraya–Watson smoothing of residuals; stacking with CV-chosen weights, e.g. stacked survival models, snippet). → hybrid (a) (head + free residual) and (c) (in-budget cross-validated stacking weight) are our own constructions, justified empirically only.
- **Already in this project:** a Gaussian-process preference learner with an RBF kernel on the SimCLR features (`nd_gp.py`; confirmed +0.013 AUC / no accuracy gain over `aw8`, `CONFIRM5_TABLE.md`) — the free-score model is its graph-kernel (kNN Laplacian, 1120 nodes) counterpart, compared here against the much stronger `lap3_k10_st4`.
