# Red team: do the recent confirmed results survive stricter evaluation and an independent implementation?

Branch `claude/redteam-20261013`. Tables are generated by `rt_report.py` from the stored result files (`new_directions/results/rt/*`, plus earlier confirmation files for audit 7); prose is hand-written. Development audits ran on seeds 8800–8819; the confirmation (seeds 8850–8884) was frozen in `PREREGISTRATION_RT1.md` before any of its cells existed. Same 168 labelled pair groups (284 frames of three growth runs) as every earlier study.

## 0. Bottom line (for the HANDOFF reader)
**No implementation error, no path-level leak; the confirmed learner gain survives four of five stricter evaluations, but it is concentrated near typed anchors, and the earlier anchor-weight gain does not survive removing near-copy anchors.** Pre-registered (RT1/RT2, seeds 8850–8884, 35 seeds, Holm):
1. *Independent re-implementation* reproduces the scores of `aw8_all`, `aw8_all_st2lr0.5`, `lap3_k10`, `lap3_k10_st4` to ≤ 2e-15 on identical label sets (graph construction and identity filter independently written); head-initialisation noise is as large as the between-cell spread (section 6) — a single fit is noisy, averages over many label sets are not.
2. *Replication of the standard endpoint:* `lap3_k10_st4` − `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` AUC +0.019 / +0.018 (A / B), accuracy +0.020 / +0.021, all Holm-significant → **robust**.
3. *Graph de-duplication* (nodes within cosine distance 0.003 of any held-out image removed): AUC +0.021 / +0.018, accuracy +0.021 / +0.016 → **robust**. *Time-blocked split* (100-frame blocks held out, 25-frame buffer removed from training, pool and graph): AUC +0.023, accuracy +0.019 → **robust**. *Anchor-strict* (typed anchors that are near-copies of held-out frames removed for all learners): AUC +0.025 / +0.030, accuracy +0.025 / +0.035 → **robust** (gain larger than on the standard endpoint).
4. *Anchor-far judgments* (both images > 0.01 from every typed anchor): AUC gain +0.003 / +0.001 (intervals include 0), accuracy +0.018 / +0.010 (Holm not significant) → **weakened**: the AUC gain over the tuned graph-free learner is carried by judgments that involve (near-copies of) typed-anchor frames; the 4× schedule adds nothing on anchor-far judgments (development).
5. *Anchor-weight check:* `aw8_all` − default head on anchor-far judgments is robust (AUC +0.025 / +0.026; so that gain is not a copy artefact when all anchors are present), but with the near-copy anchors removed it is **not supported** (AUC −0.008 / +0.003, accuracy −0.013 / −0.007): its benefit depends on typed anchors lying close to held-out frames.
Development observations (not evidence): one held-out image has a content twin in the labelled/pool images in 3 of 10 audited cells (minor); the earlier graph-learner confirmation-2 accuracy gain of `lap3_k10` over `aw8_all` is weak under several aggregations (audit 7); `lap3_k10_st4` has worse log-loss than the tuned graph-free learner on RT13 judgments and on anchor-far judgments.

## 10. What this changes in the earlier conclusions
- **Graph-learner study (G1) and selection-htr study (SH1):** the conclusions "Laplacian learner beats the tuned graph-free learner" and "selection rules ≈ 0" are reproduced under the stricter evaluations above and stand for the in-distribution endpoint (same three runs, anchors from the same runs). Add the caveat that the gain is carried by judgments adjacent to typed-anchor frames; there is no evidence of an AUC gain on frames far from every typed anchor.
- **New-directions anchor-weight finding (`aw8`/`aw8_all`):** valid when the anchors come from the same runs as the evaluated frames; not supported once near-copy anchors are removed. It should be described as "typed anchors drawn from the same growth runs", not as a general learner improvement.
- **Evaluation protocol:** random re-splits of a dense frame sequence put near-duplicates on both sides. Report, in addition to the standard endpoint, (i) the time-blocked split (gain persisted: +0.023 AUC), (ii) the anchor-far stratum, and (iii) the anchor-strict evaluation; with only three runs, generalisation to new runs/samples remains untestable until new growth data exist.
- **No correction of published numbers is needed**: no bug or leak that changes a reported number was found; the one content-twin image is not a reported-number issue at this size (not quantified further).
- **Decision for the user:** keep `lap3_k10_st4` as the working learner, but state the anchor-proximity caveat wherever the +0.02 AUC is quoted; the claim "Laplacian regularisation generalises better" needs a test on new runs (see GPU_TASKS).

## 11. Limits of this audit
- Same 168 groups and three runs: nothing here can speak to unseen sessions or samples; the time-blocked split is the closest available test.
- Thresholds (0.01, 0.003, 100/25 frames) were chosen after looking at the development seeds; the confirmation tests them once.
- Distances are computed in the repository's frozen SimCLR feature space, the same geometry the graph uses; a different encoder could rank near-duplicates differently.
