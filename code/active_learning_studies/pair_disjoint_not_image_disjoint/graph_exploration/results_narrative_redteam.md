## 1. Data geometry and identity (audit 6 + distance scale)
{{TABLE:a6}}

Reading: no held-out image is a graph node, labelled image, pool image or anchor **by path**; **by content hash** a held-out image has a content twin in the labelled/pool images in some cells (listed above), a minor identity leak of one image. `aw8_all`-family learners use the 28 outer-test ideal images as anchors by design (harmless for paired comparisons on the preference endpoint, but any external type-classification endpoint on those images is invalid for them). Typed anchors are (near-)copies of trajectory frames: the minimum distance between held-out images and anchors is ≈ 0, and the distance between *different-type* reference images has median 0.58 (5 % quantile 0.14) against 1−cos ≈ 0.0002–0.02 between a test image and its nearest training frame: the data are a dense, nearly continuous sampling of three runs. Strict de-duplication of the labelled data is not possible (see `rt_a2.py` docstring: at 1−cos ≤ 0.002 only 13–22 % of the candidate pool survives).

Reading: judgments with an image within 1−cos ≤ 0.001 of a typed anchor carry most of the learner gain (AUC +0.03–0.04 over `aw8_all`); judgments far (> 0.01) from every anchor show a small gain whose interval includes 0, and the 4× schedule adds nothing there. ## 11. Limits of this audit
- Same 168 groups and three runs: nothing here can speak to unseen sessions or samples; the time-blocked split is the closest available test.
- Thresholds (0.01, 0.003, 100/25 frames) were chosen after looking at the development seeds; the confirmation tests them once.
- Distances are computed in the repository's frozen SimCLR feature space, the same geometry the graph uses; a different encoder could rank near-duplicates differently.
