# PREREGISTRATION_E1 — frozen candidates, families and success rules (anchor-exchange study; written 2026-10-13 ~16:40 UTC, BEFORE any seed >= 8750 was run)
Development evidence: `PREREGISTRATION_E0.md` and the development screens on seeds 8700–8713 / 8700–8709 (`results/ae/dev_*`); development numbers are not evidence. Branch `claude/anchor-exchange-1013`. Learner, universes, endpoints and protocol extensions as in E0 (F1, E1–E4): learner `lap3_k10_st4`; anchor universe = the 116 non-test ideal images; the 28 outer-test ideal images are never anchors or graph nodes and give the external type-accuracy endpoint (win-rate rule, reference set = the selected anchors); main endpoint = held-out pair-group preference prediction, splits A and B, budgets 10/20/40/60, one random label set per budget shared by all arms; seed value = mean over budgets (and over the 3 random subsets for the random reference).

## Confirmation block (run once)
Confirmation 1 = seeds 8750–8784 (35), both splits, 36 anchors (about 31% of 116). Arms: random anchors (3 random subsets, averaged = the reference), the four candidates, and the shuffled-graph controls of the graph rules.

## Candidates (4 active anchor rules) and controls
C1 `htrq` (label-spreading HTR quota: 12 random anchors first, then two rounds in which half of the picks are the most HTR-like unlabelled ideal images by the spreading posterior of the anchors labelled so far and half the most ambiguous ones); control `htrq_shuf` (adjacency permuted). C2 `kcf` (greedy k-center on cosine features of the ideal images; graph-free). C3 `kcg` (greedy k-center on hop distance of the kNN graph); control `kcg_shuf`. C4 `typi` (TypiClust: k-means with 36 clusters, most typical point per cluster; graph-free).
Not carried (dev-only): `amb` (label-spreading ambiguity) and its control, `amb_shuf`.

## Families and success rules
(a) Preference endpoint. Family = {C1, C2, C3, C4} x {split A, split B} x {AUC, accuracy} = 16 tests, each = gain over random anchors (same count, same pairwise label sets), two-sided Wilcoxon over the 35 seeds, Holm over the 16. A candidate SUCCEEDS if its four gains (A/B x AUC/accuracy) are all positive, at least 3 of its 4 tests have Holm < 0.05, and — for the graph rules C1, C3 — it beats its shuffled-graph control (positive difference with uncorrected p < 0.05 for AUC or accuracy in at least one split). C2 and C4 use no graph, so a shuffled-graph control does not exist for them: if one of them succeeds it is reported as 'an active anchor rule beats random anchors', not as a graph claim. Otherwise 'not confirmed'.
(b) Exchange rate. No significance claim. Estimate and interval only: anchors {0, 12, 29, 58, 116} (0%, 10%, 25%, 50%, 100% of 116; 3 random subsets for the partial levels) x pairwise budgets {0, 10, 20, 40, 60} on the 35 confirmation seeds, both splits, overall and per type (accuracy, log-loss) and for the external endpoint; extra judgments equivalent to extra anchors from the log-axis model of `ae_analyze.exchange_table` (seed bootstrap, 1000 resamples, 95% percentile interval; transitions 0->12, 12->29, 29->58, 58->116 at pairwise budgets 10, 20, 40; 'beyond range' flagged when the equivalent exceeds 60 extra judgments). The estimator is frozen as written; if an interval is unbounded it is reported as such.
Secondary, descriptive (no Holm, no success role): log-loss, per-type metrics, external-test type accuracy of the candidates (family of 4 x 2 splits reported with Holm over those 8, labelled secondary).
Everything not listed is exploratory and will be labelled so.

## Known limitations written in advance
Same 168 pair groups / 150 ideal images in every seed (new splits, not new data): p-values are optimistic. The anchor universe is 116 ideal images, the 36-anchor budget one point of a curve. Typed labels of trajectory frames cannot be evaluated on existing data (no such labels exist); the exchange rate concerns labels of ideal images and transfers to new trajectory frames only as a hypothesis (prospective experiment in the GPU/lab list). Dev expectation (written before the confirmation): no rule beats random anchors on the preference endpoint; diversity-based rules (`kcf`, `typi`) improve the external type accuracy by about 0.02–0.04 on dev seeds.
