# Selection × Laplacian learner × HTR (branch `claude/selection-htr-20261013`)

Every table is generated by `sh_report.py` from the result JSONs (`new_directions/results/sh/*`); the text is hand-written. Same 168 pair groups as all earlier work (re-split with new seeds, **not new data**): p-values are optimistic; only the pre-registered confirmation runs (seeds 8300–8334 and 8350–8384, frozen in `PREREGISTRATION_SH1.md` before any of those cells existed) count as evidence. Development seeds: 8200–8219.

## 1. Answer
**Selection: no.** On top of the Laplacian learner `lap3_k10`, none of the four pre-registered selection candidates (`vopt_u`, graph-aware `gvopt_node3`, `typed_decisive_bald`, HTR-directed `vopt_htr4`) beats random selection on AUC or accuracy in either of two confirmation runs (35 seeds each, both splits; 0 of 16 Holm-significant tests in each; also none under the longer schedule, secondary run on confirmation-1 seeds). Point estimates are within ±0.003 AUC. This extends the earlier finding (selection advantage absorbed by a better learner) to the best learner so far. Graph-aware selection was not better than its shuffled-graph control or than the graph-free `vopt_u`.

**HTR, selection side: small, real, only on log-loss.** The HTR-directed rule `vopt_htr4` (HTR head's variance-reduction gain × 4; 42 % of the picks go to HTR instead of ≈ 25 %) improves HTR log-loss by ≈ +0.02 (Holm-significant in both splits in both confirmations) and HTR accuracy by ≈ +0.005 (significant after Holm in one of four split × confirmation cells), and the other three types lose no more than 0.005 accuracy. It does not improve overall AUC/accuracy. Whether +0.005 HTR accuracy matters to the lab is a judgement call.

**Learner side — the actual finding: the Laplacian learner needs a longer head schedule.** Training the head for 4× the repository's number of steps (`lap3_k10_st4`) beats both the best schedule I found for the Laplacian-free `aw8_all` (`aw8_all_st2lr0.5`: AUC +0.021 / +0.016 in confirmation 1, +0.016 / +0.017 in confirmation 2, splits A / B; accuracy ≈ +0.02) and the previous learner `lap3_k10` (AUC +0.006…+0.010, accuracy +0.011…+0.013, HTR log-loss +0.12) under the pre-registered rule in both confirmations (shuffled-graph control: far worse), and again in the cold-start confirmation (15 seeds, 10 random initial judgments; AUC +0.022 / +0.027 vs the tuned graph-free learner, +0.013 / +0.008 vs `lap3_k10`; all pre-registered criteria met). The same schedule change does **not** help `aw8_all` (development), so the longer schedule is something the graph regulariser makes possible (it tames overfitting of the longer fit) rather than a generic tuning gain. Relative to `aw8_all` with the repository's schedule the whole recipe gains ≈ +0.02…+0.03 AUC / accuracy and ≈ +0.03…+0.05 HTR accuracy (descriptive tables 5.1/6.1).

**HTR, learner side:** against `lap3_k10` the HTR improvement (accuracy and log-loss, both splits) replicated in both confirmations with no loss in the other types. Against the schedule-tuned Laplacian-free baseline the HTR improvement is smaller (accuracy +0.008…+0.014) and passed the pre-registered HTR rule only in confirmation 1 (see tables), so I do not claim a robust HTR advantage over a properly tuned graph-free learner.

## 2. Setting
Harness `new_directions/nd_select.py` (single-shot selection of b = 10/20/40/60 judgments from the model trained on the initial groups; the final learner is trained on initial + chosen judgments; random = mean of 3 draws; paired on identical initial model and pool). Final learners: `aw8_all` (tuned baseline), `lap3_k10` (Laplacian regulariser λ = 3 on the k = 10 cosine kNN graph of unlabelled images, three earlier pre-registered confirmations), and schedule variants (`_st<x>`: training steps × x; `_lr<x>`: learning rate × x). Protocol extension: graph-aware selectors may use the unlabelled image nodes of that graph and the typed reference images; never any outcome of a candidate or any test image. Every graph rule has a shuffled-graph control. Literature notes: `notes_selection_htr_literature.md` (search snippets only).

Selectors tried (all in `nd_select.py` / `nd_select_graph.py`): repository rules `vopt_u`, type-aware coverage (± type-only/shuffled controls), `typed_decisive_bald`, Laplacian-prior design on the candidate graph (`gvopt_lap*`); new here: information-density-weighted uncertainty (`gdens_unc`), per-type graph coverage (`gcover`, `gcover_dec`), V-optimal design under the Laplacian prior of the full node graph (`gvopt_node{1,3,10}`), HTR gain weighting (`vopt_htr{2,4}`), HTR/RT13 label-spreading "bridge" weighting (`vopt_bridge{1,3}`). Learner changes tried: per-type Laplacian weight, HTR anchor weight ×2/×4, schedule (steps, learning rate), larger/smaller λ at long schedule.

## 7. Interpretation and limits
- **What is confirmed (pre-registered, new seeds, two replications):** `lap3_k10_st4` > `aw8_all_st2lr0.5` and > `lap3_k10` on AUC and accuracy in both splits; none of four selection rules > random selection.
- **Caveats on the learner result.** (i) The schedule factor ×4 and the Laplacian-free comparison schedule were chosen on the development seeds (20 for `sdev5`, 8–12 for `sdev6`), and the Laplacian-free baseline was tuned over a small grid (steps ×2/×3/×4, lr ×0.5/×2, `st2lr0.5`) — a better-tuned graph-free learner may exist, e.g. with a different regulariser, so "graph structure is needed" is only supported by the shuffled/noisy-graph controls, not by an exhaustive search over non-graph regularisers (ridge/consistency controls were tried at the repository schedule only). (ii) Same 168 pair groups re-split: p-values optimistic. (iii) Everything is on frozen SimCLR features and the MLP head; the end-to-end model is untested (`GPU_TASKS_selection_htr.md`).
- **Why selection does not matter here.** The candidate pool is small (≈ 215 / 335 judgments in splits A / B) while budgets reach 60, so a rule can only reshuffle a large share of the pool; the learner change (+0.02…0.03 AUC) is an order of magnitude larger than any selector effect (≤ 0.003 AUC). Power: per-seed sd of the paired AUC difference ≈ 0.007–0.009, so with 35 seeds the minimum detectable effect is ≈ 0.003–0.004 AUC; the selection effects are below that, not merely unmeasured.
- **Graph-free regulariser at the long schedule (development robustness check, seeds 8200–8219, `sdev9`):** see the learner tables in section 3.4 (`cons0.3_st4`, `cons1_st4`: dropout-consistency of unlabelled nodes' scores, no graph, steps ×4).
- **Mechanism of the HTR selector:** it moves budget (picks per type: see `c1_comp`/`dev_comp`); the gain in HTR log-loss comes with no change in overall accuracy, consistent with reallocation rather than better information per label.
- **Not examined:** cold start for selectors (selection from 10 random initial judgments), selection with sequential rounds, other reference pools. The cold-start learner confirmation (SH2, 15 seeds) is reported in section 8; its HTR rows are weaker against the tuned graph-free baseline (HTR accuracy +0.012…+0.018 but not Holm-significant with 15 seeds).
