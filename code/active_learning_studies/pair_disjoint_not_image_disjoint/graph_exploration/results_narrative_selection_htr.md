## 2. Setting
Harness `new_directions/nd_select.py` (single-shot selection of b = 10/20/40/60 judgments from the model trained on the initial groups; the final learner is trained on initial + chosen judgments; random = mean of 3 draws; paired on identical initial model and pool). Final learners: `aw8_all` (tuned baseline), `lap3_k10` (Laplacian regulariser λ = 3 on the k = 10 cosine kNN graph of unlabelled images, three earlier pre-registered confirmations), and schedule variants (`_st<x>`: training steps × x; `_lr<x>`: learning rate × x). Protocol extension: graph-aware selectors may use the unlabelled image nodes of that graph and the typed reference images; never any outcome of a candidate or any test image. Every graph rule has a shuffled-graph control. Literature notes: `notes_selection_htr_literature.md` (search snippets only).

Selectors tried (all in `nd_select.py` / `nd_select_graph.py`): repository rules `vopt_u`, type-aware coverage (± type-only/shuffled controls), `typed_decisive_bald`, Laplacian-prior design on the candidate graph (`gvopt_lap*`); new here: information-density-weighted uncertainty (`gdens_unc`), per-type graph coverage (`gcover`, `gcover_dec`), V-optimal design under the Laplacian prior of the full node graph (`gvopt_node{1,3,10}`), HTR gain weighting (`vopt_htr{2,4}`), HTR/RT13 label-spreading "bridge" weighting (`vopt_bridge{1,3}`). Learner changes tried: per-type Laplacian weight, HTR anchor weight ×2/×4, schedule (steps, learning rate), larger/smaller λ at long schedule.
